# Design System for SSourcing China

## Brand Identity
- Professional, trustworthy B2B sourcing company
- Clean, international, no exaggerated claims
- Tone: authoritative but approachable, practical

## Colors
- Primary: #1A56DB (trust blue) - used for CTAs, links, primary buttons
- Primary Dark: #1E40AF - hover states
- Secondary: #0F766E (teal) - secondary accents, highlights
- Neutral-900: #111827 - headings
- Neutral-700: #374151 - body text
- Neutral-500: #6B7280 - muted text
- Neutral-100: #F3F4F6 - light backgrounds
- Neutral-50: #F9FAFB - page backgrounds
- White: #FFFFFF - cards, sections
- Success: #059669 - trust indicators

## Typography
- Font: Inter (Google Fonts)
- H1: text-4xl md:text-5xl lg:text-6xl font-bold tracking-tight
- H2: text-3xl md:text-4xl font-bold
- H3: text-xl md:text-2xl font-semibold
- Body: text-base md:text-lg leading-relaxed text-neutral-700
- Small: text-sm text-neutral-500

## Spacing
- Section padding: py-16 md:py-24
- Container max-width: max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8
- Card padding: p-6 md:p-8
- Gap between cards: gap-6 md:gap-8

## Component Styles
- Buttons: rounded-lg px-6 py-3 font-semibold transition-all duration-200
- Cards: bg-white rounded-xl shadow-sm border border-neutral-100
- Input fields: rounded-lg border border-neutral-300 px-4 py-3
- Section headings: text-center mb-12 md:mb-16

## Borders
- Subtle borders: border border-neutral-100
- Card shadows: shadow-sm hover:shadow-md transition-shadow

## Do's
- Use plenty of white space
- Use clean, sharp icons from Lucide
- Keep CTAs blue (#1A56DB)
- Use teal (#0F766E) for secondary accents
- Use grid layouts for service cards
- Keep form fields clean and simple

## Don'ts
- No overly bright or saturated colors
- No stock photos that look fake
- No exaggerated claims or superlatives
- No dark backgrounds for main content areas
- No decorative elements that don't serve a purpose
