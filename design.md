# SSourcing China - Design System

## Visual Style
- Clean, professional B2B aesthetic
- Trustworthy, international feel
- No exaggerated claims, practical tone

## Colors
- Primary: Blue (#1E40AF - tailwind blue-800) - trust, professionalism
- Primary hover: Blue-700 (#1D4ED8)
- Secondary: Slate (#0F172A - slate-900) - authority
- Accent: Amber (#D97706 - amber-600) - CTA buttons, highlights
- Background: White (#FFFFFF) and Slate-50 (#F8FAFC)
- Text: Slate-900 for headings, Slate-600 for body
- Border: Slate-200 (#E2E8F0)

## Typography
- Font: Inter (Google Fonts)
- Headings: font-bold, tracking-tight
- Body: font-normal, leading-relaxed
- Small text: text-sm, text-slate-500

## Spacing
- Section padding: py-16 md:py-24
- Container: max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8
- Card gap: gap-6 lg:gap-8
- Element spacing: space-y-4, space-y-6

## Components
- Cards: bg-white rounded-lg shadow-sm border border-slate-200
- Buttons: rounded-md, px-6 py-3, font-semibold
- CTA buttons: bg-amber-600 hover:bg-amber-700 text-white
- Secondary buttons: border border-slate-300 hover:bg-slate-50
- Section backgrounds: alternating white and slate-50

## Do's
- Use plenty of white space
- Keep text concise and scannable
- Use icons from Lucide React
- Use realistic factory/QC/shipping imagery
- Maintain consistent spacing

## Don'ts
- No exaggerated claims or hype language
- No cluttered layouts
- No bright/neon colors
- No overly decorative elements
- No dark mode (B2B prefers light)
