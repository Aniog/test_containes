# Design Guidelines - SSourcing China

## Visual Style
Clean, professional, and trustworthy B2B aesthetic. Focus on clarity, practicality, and international standards.

## Typography
- **Primary Font**: Inter (Google Fonts)
- **Headings**: Semi-bold to bold, neutral dark gray (`text-slate-900`)
- **Body**: Regular, legible line height (1.5), slate gray (`text-slate-600`)

## Color Palette
- **Primary (Blue)**: `#0F4C81` (Classic Blue/Trust)
- **Secondary (Navy)**: `#1E293B` (Professionalism)
- **Accent (Orange/Gold)**: `#D97706` (Attention to CTAs)
- **Backgrounds**:
  - Main: `#FFFFFF`
  - Muted: `#F8FAFC`
- **Text**:
  - Primary: `#0F172A`
  - Secondary: `#475569`

## Spacing & Layout
- Standard page padding: `px-4 md:px-8 max-w-7xl mx-auto`
- Section vertical spacing: `py-16 md:py-24`
- Grid gaps: `gap-8`

## Components
- **Buttons**:
  - Primary: `bg-blue-700 text-white font-semibold py-3 px-6 rounded-md hover:bg-blue-800 transition`
  - Secondary: `border-2 border-blue-700 text-blue-700 font-semibold py-3 px-6 rounded-md hover:bg-blue-50 transition`
- **Cards**: `bg-white border border-slate-200 rounded-lg shadow-sm p-6`

## Images
- Use `data-strk-img` for realistic factory, QC, and shipping visuals.
- Hero images: `16x9` ratio, professional sourcing context.
- Product/Process images: `4x3` or `3x2` ratio.
