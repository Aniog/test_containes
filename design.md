# SSourcing China Visual Design Guide

## Brand personality
Clean, trustworthy, international, and practical. The site should feel like a reliable B2B sourcing partner, not a flashy consumer brand.

## Colors
Use named Tailwind theme colors only.
- Primary: `sourcing-navy` for headers, navigation, important text, and deep trust sections.
- Accent: `sourcing-blue` for main CTAs, links, highlights, and key icons.
- Secondary accent: `sourcing-gold` for small proof points, labels, and subtle emphasis.
- Backgrounds: `sourcing-cloud`, white, and `sourcing-mist` for alternating sections.
- Text: `sourcing-ink` for body copy and `sourcing-muted` for secondary copy.

## Typography
Use Inter from Google Fonts. Keep headings direct and confident. Avoid exaggerated claims.
- Large hero headings: strong, tight, and readable.
- Section headings: concise and practical.
- Body text: clear, medium line-height, optimized for international B2B readers.

## Layout and spacing
Use generous white space, structured grids, and aligned content blocks.
- Desktop: multi-column layouts where appropriate.
- Mobile: stack sections cleanly with comfortable spacing.
- Cards: rounded corners, light borders, and subtle shadows.

## Imagery
Use realistic factory, quality control, logistics, packaging, and shipping visuals. Prefer stock image tags with contextual text references. Avoid abstract or exaggerated imagery.

## Do's
- Make CTAs easy to find.
- Make service benefits concrete and practical.
- Keep form labels clear and business-focused.
- Ensure all text has high contrast.

## Don'ts
- Do not use unrealistic promises such as guaranteed lowest price.
- Do not use low-contrast text on colored backgrounds.
- Do not overload pages with visual effects.
