# SSourcing China — Design System

## Brand Identity
- **Company**: SSourcing China
- **Tagline**: China Sourcing Agent for Global Buyers
- **Tone**: Professional, clear, practical, trustworthy, international B2B

## Color Palette
- **Primary Blue**: `#1B4F8A` — trust, reliability (Tailwind: `[#1B4F8A]`)
- **Primary Blue Dark**: `#163F6E` — hover states
- **Accent Red**: `#C0392B` — China accent, CTAs (Tailwind: `[#C0392B]`)
- **Accent Red Hover**: `#A93226`
- **Neutral Dark**: `#1A2332` — headings, body text
- **Neutral Mid**: `#4A5568` — secondary text
- **Neutral Light**: `#F7F9FC` — section backgrounds
- **White**: `#FFFFFF`
- **Border**: `#E2E8F0`
- **Success Green**: `#27AE60`

## Typography
- **Font**: Inter (Google Fonts)
- **Headings**: `font-bold` or `font-semibold`, `text-[#1A2332]`
- **H1**: `text-4xl md:text-5xl lg:text-6xl font-bold`
- **H2**: `text-3xl md:text-4xl font-bold`
- **H3**: `text-xl md:text-2xl font-semibold`
- **Body**: `text-base text-[#4A5568]`
- **Small**: `text-sm text-[#4A5568]`

## Spacing
- Section padding: `py-16 md:py-24`
- Container: `max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8`
- Card padding: `p-6 md:p-8`
- Gap between cards: `gap-6 md:gap-8`

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: `bg-[#C0392B] hover:bg-[#A93226] text-white font-semibold px-6 py-3 rounded-lg transition-colors`
- **Secondary**: `bg-[#1B4F8A] hover:bg-[#163F6E] text-white font-semibold px-6 py-3 rounded-lg transition-colors`
- **Outline**: `border-2 border-[#1B4F8A] text-[#1B4F8A] hover:bg-[#1B4F8A] hover:text-white font-semibold px-6 py-3 rounded-lg transition-colors`

### Cards
- `bg-white rounded-xl shadow-sm border border-[#E2E8F0] p-6 hover:shadow-md transition-shadow`

### Badges
- `bg-[#EBF4FF] text-[#1B4F8A] text-xs font-semibold px-3 py-1 rounded-full`
- `bg-[#FEF2F2] text-[#C0392B] text-xs font-semibold px-3 py-1 rounded-full`

### Section Backgrounds
- White: `bg-white`
- Light gray: `bg-[#F7F9FC]`
- Dark navy: `bg-[#1A2332] text-white`
- Blue: `bg-[#1B4F8A] text-white`

## Do's
- Use generous whitespace
- Keep text readable with high contrast
- Use icons alongside text for clarity
- Show trust signals prominently
- Use real-world B2B language

## Don'ts
- No exaggerated claims ("best in the world")
- No dark text on dark backgrounds
- No light text on light backgrounds
- No decorative fonts
- No cluttered layouts
