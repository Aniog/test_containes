# SSourcing China - Design System

## Brand Identity
- **Company**: SSourcing China
- **Type**: B2B China sourcing agent
- **Audience**: Overseas buyers, importers, procurement managers
- **Tone**: Professional, clear, practical, trustworthy

## Typography
- **Font**: Inter (Google Fonts, already loaded in index.html)
- **Headings**: font-weight 700-800, tight tracking
- **Body**: font-weight 400-500, normal tracking
- **Small text**: font-weight 400, text-sm

## Colors
- **Primary**: `#0f172a` (slate-900) - Deep navy for headers, trust
- **Primary Light**: `#1e40af` (blue-800) - Accent blue
- **Accent**: `#2563eb` (blue-600) - CTAs, links, interactive
- **Accent Hover**: `#1d4ed8` (blue-700)
- **Background**: `#ffffff` (white)
- **Background Alt**: `#f8fafc` (slate-50)
- **Background Dark**: `#0f172a` (slate-900)
- **Text Primary**: `#0f172a` (slate-900)
- **Text Secondary**: `#475569` (slate-600)
- **Text Muted**: `#64748b` (slate-500)
- **Border**: `#e2e8f0` (slate-200)
- **Border Light**: `#f1f5f9` (slate-100)
- **Success**: `#16a34a` (green-600)
- **Warning**: `#ca8a04` (yellow-600)

## Spacing
- **Section padding**: py-16 md:py-24
- **Container**: max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8
- **Card padding**: p-6 md:p-8
- **Gap between items**: gap-6 md:gap-8

## Layout
- **Max width**: max-w-7xl (1280px)
- **Grid**: grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-3
- **Two column**: grid-cols-1 lg:grid-cols-2

## Components
- **Buttons**: rounded-lg, px-6 py-3, font-medium, transition
- **Cards**: rounded-xl, border border-slate-200, bg-white, shadow-sm
- **Inputs**: rounded-lg, border border-slate-300, px-4 py-3, focus:ring-2 focus:ring-blue-600
- **Badges**: rounded-full, px-3 py-1, text-xs font-medium

## Do's
- Use plenty of white space
- Keep text concise and scannable
- Use icons to support text
- Maintain consistent spacing
- Use real factory/QC/shipping imagery

## Don'ts
- No exaggerated claims or hype language
- No cluttered layouts
- No bright/neon colors
- No overly decorative elements
- No stock photos that look fake
