# Visual Style Guide - SSourcing China

## Typography
- Primary Font: Inter (Google Fonts)
- Headings: Bold, primary color (#1a365d), large tracking.
- Body: Regular, dark gray (#374151), line-height 1.6.

## Colors
- Primary: #1a365d (Deep Navy) - Trust, professionalism, B2B.
- Secondary: #f6ad55 (Muted Orange) - Call to action, energy, accent.
- Background: #f8fafc (Light Gray/Blue) - Clean, modern.
- Text: #1f2937 (Slate Gray) - Readability.

## Borders & Spacing
- Rounded Corners: `rounded-lg` (8px) for cards and buttons.
- Spacing: Consistent use of Tailwind spacing scale (e.g., `py-12`, `py-20` for sections).
- Container: Max-width `max-w-7xl` with `mx-auto` and `px-4`.

## Visual Elements
- Shadow: `shadow-sm` for cards, `shadow-md` for hover states.
- Buttons: Primary button with navy background and white text. Secondary button for secondary CTA.
- Imagery: Realistic, professional photos of production, quality control, shipping, and business meetings.

## Do's
- Use plenty of white space.
- Keep the design clean and international.
- Use clear, legible font sizes (minimum 16px for body).
- Use Lucide icons for visual cues.

## Don'ts
- Use overly bright or neon colors.
- Use low-contrast text.
- Over-clutter the layout with too many elements.
