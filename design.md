# SSourcing China - Design System

## Brand Identity
- **Company**: SSourcing China
- **Industry**: B2B Sourcing Agent (China-based)
- **Tone**: Professional, trustworthy, international, practical

## Colors
- **Primary**: `brand-navy` (#1B2B4B) - deep navy for authority and trust
- **Primary Accent**: `brand-blue` (#2563EB) - action blue for CTAs and links
- **Secondary**: `brand-orange` (#F97316) - warm orange for highlights and urgency
- **Neutral Dark**: `brand-slate` (#334155) - body text
- **Neutral Light**: `brand-gray` (#F8FAFC) - section backgrounds
- **Border**: `brand-border` (#E2E8F0) - subtle borders
- **White**: #FFFFFF - cards and content areas

## Typography
- **Font Family**: Inter (Google Font)
- **Headings**: font-bold, tracking-tight
  - H1: text-4xl md:text-5xl lg:text-6xl
  - H2: text-3xl md:text-4xl
  - H3: text-xl md:text-2xl
  - H4: text-lg md:text-xl
- **Body**: text-base (16px), text-brand-slate, leading-relaxed
- **Small**: text-sm, text-slate-500

## Spacing
- Section padding: py-16 md:py-24
- Container: max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8
- Card padding: p-6 md:p-8
- Gap between cards: gap-6 md:gap-8

## Components
- **Buttons Primary**: bg-brand-blue text-white px-6 py-3 rounded-lg font-semibold hover:bg-blue-700 transition-colors
- **Buttons Secondary**: border-2 border-brand-blue text-brand-blue px-6 py-3 rounded-lg font-semibold hover:bg-brand-blue hover:text-white transition-colors
- **Cards**: bg-white rounded-xl shadow-sm border border-brand-border p-6 hover:shadow-md transition-shadow
- **Section Headings**: text-center with subtitle in text-slate-600

## Layout
- Desktop: multi-column grids (2-3-4 columns)
- Mobile: single column stacking
- Max content width: 1280px (max-w-7xl)
- Navigation: sticky top, white bg with shadow

## Do's
- Use plenty of whitespace
- Keep CTAs prominent and consistent
- Use icons (Lucide) to support text
- Use stock images for factory, QC, shipping contexts
- Maintain consistent card styling

## Don'ts
- No dark mode (light theme only)
- No overly decorative elements
- No exaggerated marketing claims
- No low-contrast text
- No magic hex values outside the defined palette
