# Visual Style Decisions: SSourcing China

## Tone & Concept
The brand style is clean, trustworthy, international, and professional B2B. It conveys reliability and practical expertise in China sourcing, factory verification, and quality control.

## Colors
- **Primary**: Deep Blue (#0F172A - slate-900) - for trust, corporate stability.
- **Secondary**: Blue Accent (#2563EB - blue-600) - for CTAs, active states, and highlights.
- **Backgrounds**: 
  - Main background: Pure White (#FFFFFF), Very Light Gray (#F8FAFC - slate-50).
  - Subtle section background: Light Gray (#F1F5F9 - slate-100).
- **Text**: 
  - Primary text: Dark Blue/Gray (#0F172A - slate-900).
  - Secondary/Muted text: Gray (#64748B - slate-500).

## Typography
- Main layout fonts: Inter for clean, readable, professional UI. 

## Borders & Spacing
- Borders: use `border-slate-200` for cards and dividers.
- Rounded corners: `rounded-lg` or `rounded-xl` for cards, `rounded-md` for buttons and smaller UI elements. Avoid overly rounded `rounded-full` except for badges and avatars.

## Do's and Don'ts
- **DO** use generous padding in sections (`py-16 md:py-24`).
- **DO** ensure high contrast for all text (e.g. `text-slate-900` on `bg-white`).
- **DO** use the designated blue accent exclusively for primary actions.
- **DON'T** use overly playful or casual typography.
- **DON'T** clutter layouts, maintain negative space.
- **DON'T** use low contrast unreadable text.
