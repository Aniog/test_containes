# SSourcing China - Visual Style Guide

## Typography
- Primary font: Inter (Google Font)
- Headings: font-bold (700) or font-semibold (600), clean and modern
- Body: font-normal (400), size text-base (16px) or text-lg (18px)
- Use leading-relaxed or leading-normal for body text readability

## Colors
- Brand navy: #1e3a5f (brand-500) - primary navy, used for header, footer, headings, buttons
- Brand dark: #0f1a2d (brand-800) - dark backgrounds, footer
- Accent orange: #f97316 (accent-500) - CTAs, highlights, active elements
- Background light: #f8fafc - page background
- Background white: #ffffff - card backgrounds
- Text dark: #1e293b - body text
- Text muted: #64748b - secondary text
- Border: #e2e8f0 - subtle borders
- Success green: #16a34a - trust indicators, checkmarks

## Spacing
- Section padding: py-16 md:py-24
- Container max-width: max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8
- Gap between cards: gap-6 md:gap-8
- Stack spacing: space-y-4, space-y-6

## Visual Style
- Clean, professional B2B aesthetic
- Cards with white background, subtle shadow (shadow-sm, shadow-md)
- Rounded corners: rounded-lg (8px) for cards, rounded-md (6px) for buttons
- Primary buttons: bg-brand-500 text-white with hover:bg-brand-600
- Accent buttons: bg-accent-500 text-white with hover:bg-accent-600
- Links: text-brand-500 hover:text-accent-500
- Dividers: thin border-t border-gray-100
- Icons: lucide-react, size 5-6, colors matching brand or accent

## Layout
- Sticky header with white background, shadow-sm
- Footer with dark navy background (brand-800), light text
- Two-column layouts for service descriptions and process steps
- Three-column grids for service cards and trust points
- Responsive: single column on mobile, 2-3 columns on desktop

## Sections
- Alternating section backgrounds: white and gray-50
- Section headings: text-3xl md:text-4xl font-bold text-center mb-4
- Section subheadings: text-lg text-muted-foreground text-center max-w-2xl mx-auto mb-12
- CTA sections: bg-brand-500 or brand-600 with white text

## Do's
- Use consistent spacing and alignment
- Keep plenty of white space
- Use professional, realistic stock images for factories, QC, shipping
- Make CTAs prominent with accent orange
- Use icons to illustrate services and process steps
- Keep text concise and benefit-focused

## Don'ts
- Don't use flashy gradients or animations
- Don't use casual or informal language
- Don't overcrowd sections with too much text
- Don't use low-quality or generic stock photos
- Don't make exaggerated claims ("best", "guaranteed", etc.)
