# Velmora Fine Jewelry - Design System

## Typography
- **Headings & Product Names**: `Cormorant Garamond` (Serif). Product names uppercase with `tracking-[0.15em]`.
- **Body & UI**: `Inter` (Sans-serif).

## Palette
- **Background**: `#F9F8F6` (hsl(20, 14%, 97%)) - Warm light neutral.
- **Foreground**: `#231F1C` (hsl(20, 14%, 12%)) - Deep charcoal.
- **Primary (Gold)**: `#91763B` (hsl(38, 42%, 40%)) - Muted gold tone for accents and buttons.
- **Accent**: `#F2EFE9` (hsl(38, 42%, 90%)) - Soft beige for backgrounds and hover.

## Key Visual Elements
- **Borders**: Hairline thin dividers (`border-border`).
- **Shadows**: Soft, almost invisible elevation.
- **Transitions**: Smooth `ease-in-out` hover effects.
- **Spacing**: Generous whitespace for editorial feel.
