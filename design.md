# SSourcing China - Design System

## Brand Identity
- **Company**: SSourcing China - B2B sourcing agent for global buyers
- **Tone**: Professional, trustworthy, clear, practical, international
- **Target**: Overseas buyers sourcing products from China

## Color Palette
- **Primary Blue**: `#1B4D9E` (trust, professionalism) - `blue-800`
- **Primary Dark**: `#0F2D5E` (headlines, nav) - `blue-900`
- **Accent Orange**: `#E87C3E` (CTAs, highlights) - `orange-500`
- **Accent Light**: `#F5A623` (hover states) - `amber-500`
- **Dark Text**: `#1A1A2E` (body text) - `slate-900`
- **Medium Text**: `#4A5568` (secondary text) - `gray-600`
- **Light Background**: `#F7FAFC` (sections) - `gray-50`
- **White**: `#FFFFFF` (cards, primary bg)
- **Light Blue BG**: `#EBF4FF` (accent sections) - `blue-50`
- **Border**: `#E2E8F0` (subtle borders) - `gray-200`

## Typography
- **Headings**: Inter (weight 700, 600)
- **Body**: Inter (weight 400, 500)
- **Hero H1**: `text-4xl md:text-5xl lg:text-6xl` leading-tight
- **Section H2**: `text-3xl md:text-4xl` leading-snug
- **Card Title**: `text-xl md:text-2xl`
- **Body Large**: `text-lg` leading-relaxed
- **Body**: `text-base` leading-normal
- **Small/Caption**: `text-sm`

## Spacing
- **Section Padding**: `py-16 md:py-24`
- **Container**: `max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8`
- **Card Padding**: `p-6 md:p-8`
- **Gap Between Cards**: `gap-6 md:gap-8`

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: `bg-orange-500 hover:bg-orange-600 text-white font-semibold px-8 py-4 rounded-lg shadow-md`
- **Secondary**: `bg-blue-800 hover:bg-blue-900 text-white font-semibold px-6 py-3 rounded-lg`
- **Outline**: `border-2 border-blue-800 text-blue-800 hover:bg-blue-800 hover:text-white px-6 py-3 rounded-lg`

### Cards
- **Standard**: `bg-white rounded-xl shadow-sm border border-gray-200 p-6 hover:shadow-md transition-shadow`
- **Feature Card**: `bg-white rounded-xl shadow-sm p-8 text-center border border-gray-100`

### Navigation
- **Desktop**: White background, sticky, shadow on scroll, blue-900 text
- **Mobile**: Hamburger menu, full-screen overlay

### Footer
- **Background**: `bg-gray-900 text-gray-300`
- **Links**: `text-gray-400 hover:text-white`

## Stock Images
Use data-strk-img attributes for realistic B2B visuals:
- Factory inspections and warehouse scenes
- Quality control and product testing
- Shipping containers and logistics
- Team collaboration and business meetings
- Product sourcing and samples

## Layout Principles
- Clean whitespace between sections
- Maximum content width: 7xl (1280px)
- Cards in responsive grids: 1 col mobile, 2 col tablet, 3-4 col desktop
- Alternating section backgrounds (white / gray-50)
- Consistent section headers: centered, blue-800 title, gray-600 subtitle

## Don'ts
- Don't use overly bright or saturated colors
- Don't use exaggerated claims or superlatives
- Don't use decorative fonts - keep it professional
- Don't use low-contrast text combinations
- Don't overcrowd sections - maintain breathing room
