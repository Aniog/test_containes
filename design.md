# SSourcing China Design System

## Brand feel
Clean, trustworthy, international, practical B2B. The site should feel like a reliable sourcing operations partner rather than a flashy marketplace.

## Colors
- Primary navy: `brand-navy` (`#0F2A43`) for headers, navigation, high-importance text, and deep backgrounds.
- Action blue: `brand-blue` (`#1D6FA3`) for primary CTAs, active states, and important links.
- Soft blue: `brand-soft` (`#EAF4FA`) for calm section backgrounds and process blocks.
- Trust green: `brand-green` (`#1F7A5A`) for quality, verification, and success accents.
- Warm amber: `brand-amber` (`#C9842B`) for subtle highlights only.
- Ink: `brand-ink` (`#172331`) for body text.
- Muted: `brand-muted` (`#65758A`) for supporting copy.
- Border: `brand-border` (`#D9E2EA`) for card and form boundaries.
- Page: `brand-page` (`#F7FAFC`) for the main background.

## Typography
Use Inter from Google Fonts. Headings should be strong and direct, with tight line height. Body copy should be readable, practical, and not overly promotional.

Example Tailwind classes:
- Main headline: `text-4xl md:text-6xl font-semibold tracking-tight text-brand-navy`
- Section heading: `text-3xl md:text-4xl font-semibold tracking-tight text-brand-navy`
- Body copy: `text-base md:text-lg leading-8 text-brand-muted`

## Layout and spacing
- Use generous whitespace and clear section separation.
- Containers should use `max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8`.
- Cards should use rounded corners, subtle borders, and soft shadows.
- Desktop layouts should use 2–4 columns where appropriate; mobile should stack cleanly.

## Components
- Buttons: rounded, confident, high-contrast. Primary buttons use action blue with white text.
- Cards: white background, navy/ink text, explicit readable foreground colors.
- Forms: clear labels, large inputs, visible borders, helpful helper text.
- Badges: subtle backgrounds with readable text.

## Imagery
Use realistic factory, quality inspection, manufacturing, warehouse, container shipping, and business coordination visuals through the Strikingly image tagging system. Avoid generic lifestyle photos.

## Do's
- Be specific about services and process.
- Keep claims practical and credible.
- Make inquiry CTAs easy to find.
- Keep all text high-contrast and readable.

## Don'ts
- Do not use exaggerated claims such as guaranteed lowest price or risk-free sourcing.
- Do not use low-contrast text on colored backgrounds.
- Do not hardcode image URLs.
