# Velmora Fine Jewelry - Design System

## Typography
- Headings & Product Names: Cormorant Garamond (Serif), elegant, UPPERCASE with wide letter-spacing for product names.
- Body & UI: Inter or Manrope (clean sans-serif).

## Colors
- Base: Deep refined neutral/dark gray or soft editorial beige (We will use a warm editorial beige/cream background `#faf8f5` with deep charcoal/espresso text `#2c2a29` and rich gold accents `#c5a059`).
- Primary Text: Deep Charcoal/Espresso (`#2c2a29`)
- Background: Soft Cream/Alabaster (`#faf8f5`)
- Accent: Rich Gold/Warm Brass (`#c5a059` / `#b38c4b`)
- Secondary Background: Soft Taupe (`#eae6df`)
- White: `#ffffff`

## UI Elements
- Dividers: Thin hairline dividers (`border-[#eae6df]`)
- Whitespace: Generous spacing, large editorial imagery.
- Buttons: Solid or outlined accent. Premium feel with subtle hover transitions.
- Shadows: Soft, elegant shadows.
- Animations: Smooth, subtle fade-ins.

## Do's
- Keep it quiet luxury, warm, and editorial.
- Ensure strong contrast and accessibility.
- Use elegant serif for headings.

## Don'ts
- No loud or generic e-commerce looks.
- No discount aesthetics.
