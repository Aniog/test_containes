# SSourcing China - Design System

## Visual Style
Clean, trustworthy, international B2B aesthetic. Professional and practical tone.

## Colors
- Primary: Navy blue (#1e3a5f) - trust, professionalism
- Primary hover: Darker navy (#162d4a)
- Accent: Gold/amber (#d4a843) - premium, quality
- Background: White (#ffffff)
- Surface: Light gray (#f8f9fa)
- Text primary: Dark slate (#1a1a2e)
- Text secondary: Medium gray (#6b7280)
- Border: Light gray (#e5e7eb)
- Success: Green (#10b981)

## Typography
- Font: Inter (Google Fonts)
- Headings: font-semibold, tracking-tight
- Body: font-normal, leading-relaxed
- Small text: text-sm, text-muted-foreground

## Spacing
- Section padding: py-16 md:py-24
- Container: max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8
- Card gap: gap-6 or gap-8
- Element spacing: space-y-4, space-y-6

## Components
- Cards: bg-white rounded-xl shadow-sm border border-border p-6
- Buttons: rounded-lg, px-6 py-3, font-medium
- Forms: rounded-lg border border-input, focus:ring-2 focus:ring-primary
- Badges: rounded-full, px-3 py-1, text-xs font-medium

## Don'ts
- No bright/neon colors
- No overly decorative elements
- No exaggerated claims or hype language
- No cluttered layouts
- No low-contrast text
