## Visual Style
- Clean and spacious layouts
- Subtle shadows for depth
- Rounded corners (8px default)
- Professional photography style (factory, shipping, quality control)
- Clear hierarchy with whitespace
- International feel with neutral backgrounds

## Do's
- Use consistent spacing throughout
- Maintain clear visual hierarchy
- Use professional, muted colors for backgrounds
- Keep text readable with sufficient contrast
- Use stock images for factory, QC, and shipping scenes

## Don'ts
- Don't use overly bright or flashy colors
- Don't overcrowd sections with too much content
- Don't use decorative fonts for body text
- Don't use low-contrast text combinations
- Don't use generic stock photos that look fake
