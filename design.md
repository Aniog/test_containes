# SSourcing China Design System

## Brand direction
A clean, trustworthy, international B2B website for overseas buyers who need practical sourcing support in China. The experience should feel credible, structured, and conversion-focused rather than promotional or flashy.

## Visual style
- Overall mood: professional, reliable, calm, precise
- Layout: spacious sections, clear hierarchy, business-friendly grids
- Desktop: strong two-column hero and multi-column information blocks
- Mobile: stacked sections with generous spacing and readable forms
- Imagery: realistic factory audits, product inspection, production follow-up, packaging, and shipping coordination

## Color system
Use Tailwind semantic-style utility choices mapped through standard classes.
- Primary surfaces: `bg-slate-950`, `bg-slate-900`, `bg-white`
- Secondary surfaces: `bg-slate-100`, `bg-slate-50`, `bg-emerald-50`
- Text: `text-slate-950`, `text-slate-800`, `text-slate-700`, `text-slate-600`, `text-white`
- Accent actions: `bg-emerald-600`, `hover:bg-emerald-500`, `text-emerald-700`, `ring-emerald-200`
- Borders: `border-slate-200`, `border-slate-300`, `border-white/15`

## Typography
- Font: Inter from Google Fonts
- Main heading example: `text-4xl md:text-5xl lg:text-6xl font-semibold tracking-tight`
- Section heading example: `text-3xl md:text-4xl font-semibold tracking-tight`
- Body copy example: `text-base md:text-lg leading-7 text-slate-600`
- Small labels example: `text-sm font-medium uppercase tracking-[0.18em]`

## Spacing and sizing
- Section padding: `py-16 md:py-24`
- Container width: `max-w-7xl mx-auto px-6 md:px-8`
- Card padding: `p-6 md:p-8`
- Rounded corners: `rounded-2xl` or `rounded-3xl`
- Shadows: `shadow-sm`, `shadow-xl shadow-slate-950/10` only where needed

## Components
- Buttons should look confident and simple, with solid primary and outline secondary variants
- Cards should use white or dark navy surfaces with explicit readable foreground colors
- Badges should be subtle and business-like, not playful
- Stats should be concise and credible
- Forms should be clean, structured, and easy to scan

## Do
- Keep content practical and specific
- Use explicit readable text colors on every non-default surface
- Maintain strong alignment between headings, copy, and CTAs
- Prefer simple icon use and restrained motion

## Don’t
- Don’t use flashy gradients as the main identity
- Don’t use exaggerated marketing language
- Don’t crowd desktop sections into narrow single-column layouts
- Don’t leave muted text too faint to read
