# Velmora Fine Jewelry Design System

## Brand direction
Velmora should feel like quiet luxury: editorial, warm, refined, and premium without looking intimidating. The visual tone should flatter gold jewelry and make product photography feel luminous.

## Color system
Use one consistent palette across the full storefront.

- Background: deep espresso-plum for immersive premium sections. Tailwind token idea: `bg-noir`
- Surface: warm ivory for product and editorial cards. Token idea: `bg-ivory`
- Surface alt: muted stone for soft contrast blocks. Token idea: `bg-stone`
- Text primary on light surfaces: inky brown. Token idea: `text-ink`
- Text primary on dark surfaces: warm cream. Token idea: `text-cream`
- Accent metallic: muted champagne gold. Token idea: `bg-gold`, `text-gold`, `border-gold`
- Divider: subtle hairline taupe. Token idea: `border-line`

## Typography
- Headings and logo: `Cormorant Garamond`
- Body/UI: `Inter`
- Product names: uppercase with generous tracking, small serif or uppercase sans presentation depending on context

## Layout and spacing
- Use generous whitespace.
- Prefer roomy sections such as `py-16 md:py-24`.
- Keep content in elegant max widths such as `max-w-7xl`.
- Use thin dividers and restrained shadows.
- Desktop should feel editorial and balanced, not stacked like mobile.

## Components
- Buttons should feel premium: either dark filled with light text or transparent with refined gold border.
- Cards should use subtle radius and soft shadows, not playful rounded corners.
- Hover transitions should be soft and understated.
- Sticky navigation starts transparent over hero and becomes solid once scrolling.
- Trust bar should be thin and understated.

## Imagery
- Use warm, close-up jewelry photography.
- Favor neutral, dark, or softly lit backgrounds.
- Product images should feel polished and premium.
- Editorial sections can use richer dark backgrounds with luminous highlights.

## Do
- Use explicit readable foreground colors on every surface.
- Keep the overall feeling warm, elevated, and modern.
- Let typography and whitespace carry the luxury feel.

## Don't
- Do not use discount-style badges, bright sales colors, or loud gradients.
- Do not use chunky borders or cartoonish shadows.
- Do not mix multiple color directions.
- Do not let text inherit into low-contrast states.
