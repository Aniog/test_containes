# SSourcing China — Design System

## Brand Identity
- Company: SSourcing China
- Tagline: China Sourcing Agent for Global Buyers
- Tone: Professional, trustworthy, international, practical

## Color Palette
- Primary Blue: #1B4F8A (deep navy-blue — trust, reliability)
- Primary Blue Light: #2563EB (accent blue for CTAs)
- Secondary Red: #C0392B (China accent — used sparingly)
- Neutral Dark: #1A2332 (headings, dark text)
- Neutral Mid: #4B5563 (body text)
- Neutral Light: #F8FAFC (section backgrounds)
- White: #FFFFFF
- Border: #E2E8F0
- Success Green: #16A34A

## Typography
- Font Family: Inter (Google Fonts)
- Heading 1: text-4xl md:text-5xl font-bold text-[#1A2332]
- Heading 2: text-3xl font-bold text-[#1A2332]
- Heading 3: text-xl font-semibold text-[#1A2332]
- Body: text-base text-[#4B5563]
- Small/Caption: text-sm text-[#6B7280]

## Spacing
- Section padding: py-16 md:py-24
- Container: max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8
- Card padding: p-6 md:p-8
- Gap between cards: gap-6 md:gap-8

## Navbar
- bg-white border-b border-[#E2E8F0] sticky top-0 z-50
- Logo: font-bold text-[#1B4F8A]
- Nav links: text-[#4B5563] hover:text-[#2563EB]
- CTA button in nav: primary style

## Footer
- bg-[#1A2332] text-white
- Links: text-[#94A3B8] hover:text-white

## Do's
- Use generous whitespace between sections
- Use icons (Lucide) alongside text for visual clarity
- Use subtle shadows on cards, not heavy drop shadows
- Keep CTAs prominent and action-oriented
- Use real-looking data in case studies (no exaggerated claims)

## Don'ts
- No dark mode toggle (light-only site)
- No neon or overly bright colors
- No exaggerated claims ("best in the world", "100% guaranteed")
- No cluttered layouts
