# Velmora Fine Jewelry — Design System

## Brand Essence
Quiet luxury, warm, editorial. Premium demi-fine jewelry. NOT loud, NOT discount, NOT generic.

## Color Palette
- **Background**: `#FAF7F2` (warm ivory/cream) — sitewide page background
- **Surface**: `#FFFFFF` — cards, drawers, dropdowns
- **Primary / Accent**: `#C79A5E` (warm gold) — CTAs, accent borders, star ratings, active states
- **Primary Hover**: `#B8894D` (deeper gold)
- **Text Primary**: `#1A1A1A` (near-black) — headings, body
- **Text Secondary**: `#6B6358` (warm gray) — captions, labels, secondary text
- **Text Muted**: `#9C9488` (light warm gray) — placeholders, disabled
- **Border**: `#E8E2D8` (warm light beige) — dividers, card borders
- **Border Light**: `#F0EBE4` (very light) — subtle separators
- **Hero Overlay**: `#1A1A1A` with 40% opacity

## Typography
- **Headings / Product Names**: `Cormorant Garamond` — serif, elegant, weight 500-600
- **Product names in UPPERCASE with letter-spacing: 0.08em**
- **Body / UI**: `Inter` — clean sans-serif, weight 300-500
- **Font sizes**: h1 (3.5rem/4rem), h2 (2.5rem), h3 (1.75rem), h4 (1.25rem), body (0.9375rem), small (0.8125rem)
- **Line height**: headings 1.1, body 1.6

## Spacing
- **Section padding**: py-20 md:py-28 (desktop), py-16 (mobile)
- **Container max-width**: max-w-7xl mx-auto px-6
- **Grid gaps**: gap-6 md:gap-8
- **Card padding**: p-6

## Borders & Dividers
- Thin hairline dividers: `border-t border-[#E8E2D8]`
- Card borders: `border border-[#E8E2D8]` — subtle, not heavy
- Rounded corners: rounded-sm (2px) for cards, rounded-none for buttons

## Shadows
- Soft, elegant: `shadow-sm` for cards, `shadow-md` for elevated elements
- Hover: `shadow-lg` with smooth transition

## Buttons
- **Primary**: bg-[#C79A5E] text-white, hover:bg-[#B8894D], uppercase, tracking-widest, text-xs, px-10 py-4
- **Outline**: border border-[#C79A5E] text-[#C79A5E], hover:bg-[#C79A5E] hover:text-white
- **Ghost**: no border, text-[#1A1A1A], hover:text-[#C79A5E]
- All buttons: transition-all duration-300, uppercase, tracking-[0.08em], text-xs

## Transitions & Animations
- Default: `transition-all duration-300 ease-in-out`
- Hover: subtle opacity shifts, smooth color changes
- Cart drawer: slide from right, 300ms
- Product card hover: image scale 1.03, shadow increase

## Product Images
- Ratio: 4:3 for product cards, 3:4 for detail gallery
- Second image reveal on hover (bestsellers)
- Warm gold jewelry on dark or neutral backgrounds

## DO's
- Generous whitespace
- Thin, elegant dividers
- Uppercase product names with wide letter-spacing
- Serif for headings, sans-serif for body
- Warm, editorial feel
- Gold accent color consistently applied

## DON'Ts
- No loud colors or bright primaries
- No heavy borders
- No discount/coupon-feeling elements
- No generic e-commerce patterns
- No emoji
- No bright red or blue accents
