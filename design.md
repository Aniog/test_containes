# SSourcing China Design Guide

## Brand direction
- Clean, trustworthy, international B2B presentation
- Practical and professional tone with no hype
- Layout should feel structured, calm, and conversion-focused
- Use realistic visuals related to factories, inspections, production, packaging, and shipping

## Recommended Tailwind style language
- Page background: `bg-slate-50`
- Primary surfaces: `bg-white`
- Alternate sections: `bg-slate-100`
- Main text: `text-slate-900`
- Secondary text: `text-slate-600`
- Accent / primary actions: `bg-blue-700 text-white`
- Accent hover: `hover:bg-blue-800`
- Borders: `border-slate-200`
- Soft highlights: `bg-blue-50 text-blue-800`
- Trust / support accents: `bg-amber-50 text-amber-800`

## Typography
- Use Inter for all text
- Hero headline should feel confident and concise
- Section headings should be direct and business-oriented
- Body copy should stay short, scannable, and practical

## Spacing and layout
- Use generous whitespace on desktop and balanced padding on mobile
- Prefer max-width containers such as `max-w-7xl` and `max-w-6xl`
- Use rounded cards with light borders and subtle shadows
- Keep forms and CTA sections visually prominent but not flashy

## Components
- Buttons should be solid, clear, and easy to spot
- Cards should use readable foreground colors explicitly
- Navigation should feel corporate and simple
- Use badges for proof points, process labels, and industry tags

## Do
- Keep visuals grounded in real sourcing operations
- Highlight process clarity, communication, and risk reduction
- Use short lists and proof-oriented messaging
- Make inquiry CTAs visible across the site

## Don't
- Do not use exaggerated claims or overly salesy headlines
- Do not use low-contrast text
- Do not overload pages with decorative effects
- Do not use trendy startup styling that feels casual or consumer-focused
