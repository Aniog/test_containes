# SSourcing China - B2B Website Design System

## Visual Style
- Clean, professional, trustworthy B2B design
- International business aesthetic with factory/QC/shipping imagery
- White backgrounds with subtle gray sections for contrast
- Blue primary color for trust and professionalism

## Colors
- Primary: blue-700 (#1d4ed8) - trust, professionalism
- Primary hover: blue-800 (#1e40af)
- Primary light: blue-50 (#eff6ff)
- Secondary: slate-800 (#1e293b) - headings, dark text
- Text: slate-600 (#475569) - body text
- Muted: slate-400 (#94a3b8) - secondary text
- Background: white (#ffffff)
- Section background: slate-50 (#f8fafc)
- Accent: amber-500 (#f59e0b) - CTAs, highlights
- Success: green-600 (#16a34a)
- Border: slate-200 (#e2e8f0)

## Typography
- Font: Inter (Google Fonts)
- Headings: font-bold, tracking-tight
- H1: text-4xl md:text-5xl lg:text-6xl
- H2: text-3xl md:text-4xl
- H3: text-xl md:text-2xl
- Body: text-base md:text-lg
- Small: text-sm

## Spacing
- Section padding: py-16 md:py-24
- Container: max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8
- Card padding: p-6 md:p-8
- Gap between elements: gap-6 md:gap-8

## Components
- Buttons: rounded-lg, px-6 py-3, font-semibold
- Cards: rounded-xl, border border-slate-200, bg-white, shadow-sm
- Inputs: rounded-lg, border border-slate-300, px-4 py-3
- Badges: rounded-full, px-3 py-1, text-sm

## Do's
- Use plenty of white space
- Keep text concise and scannable
- Use icons to support text content
- Show real factory/QC/shipping imagery
- Maintain consistent spacing and alignment

## Don'ts
- Don't use bright or neon colors
- Don't overcrowd sections with text
- Don't use decorative fonts
- Don't use exaggerated claims or hype language
- Don't use dark backgrounds for main content
