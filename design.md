# SSourcing China Visual Design System

## Brand direction
SSourcing China should feel clean, trustworthy, international, and practical for overseas B2B buyers. The design should communicate factory access, quality control discipline, and shipping coordination without exaggerated claims.

## Colors
- Primary navy: `brand-navy` for headers, navigation, key text, and high-trust surfaces.
- Primary blue: `brand-blue` for CTA buttons, links, and active states.
- Soft blue: `brand-sky` for light section backgrounds and subtle highlights.
- Accent amber: `brand-amber` for measured emphasis, status badges, and process markers.
- Neutral surfaces: white cards on `brand-mist` page backgrounds.
- Text: use `brand-ink` for primary copy and `brand-muted` for secondary text.

## Typography
- Use Inter from Google Fonts for a modern international B2B feel.
- Large headlines should be confident, tight, and easy to scan. Example: `text-4xl md:text-6xl font-semibold tracking-tight`.
- Body copy should be practical and readable. Example: `text-base md:text-lg leading-7`.

## Spacing and layout
- Use generous section spacing: `py-16 md:py-24`.
- Main containers: `max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8`.
- Use multi-column layouts on desktop and stacked layouts on mobile.
- Cards should use `rounded-2xl border border-slate-200 bg-white shadow-sm`.

## Components
- Primary CTA: blue background, white text, rounded full, practical label.
- Secondary CTA: white or transparent, navy text, subtle border.
- Badges: small, uppercase or concise, with strong contrast.
- Forms: white card, clear labels, readable placeholders, visible focus states.

## Images
- Use realistic factory, inspection, packaging, container, logistics, and product category visuals.
- Avoid lifestyle-heavy visuals, exaggerated hero imagery, or generic abstract graphics.

## Do
- Keep language clear and practical.
- Make all text explicitly readable against its background.
- Use consistent card spacing and aligned section headings.

## Don't
- Do not use low-contrast text.
- Do not overuse bright colors or decorative effects.
- Do not make exaggerated claims such as guaranteed lowest price or risk-free sourcing.
