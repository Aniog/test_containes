# Velmora Fine Jewelry — Design System

## Brand Identity
- Mood: Quiet luxury, warm, editorial. Premium demi-fine gold jewelry.
- NOT loud, NOT discount-looking, NOT generic e-commerce.

## Color Palette
- Cream: #FAF7F2 (page background)
- Sand: #F0EBE3 (card backgrounds, subtle sections)
- Gold: #C8A96E (primary accent, CTAs, icons)
- Gold Light: #E0D0A8 (dividers, subtle accents)
- Gold Dark: #A68B4B (hover states, active)
- Charcoal: #2C2C2C (secondary text)
- Ink: #1A1A1A (primary text)
- Stone: #6B6B6B (muted text, placeholders)
- Pearl: #F5F1EB (alternate warm section bg)
- Rose: #B88A7E (secondary accent, subtle warmth)

## Typography
- Headings: Cormorant Garamond (serif) — elegant, editorial
- Body/UI: Inter (sans-serif) — clean, readable
- Product names: UPPERCASE with wide letter-spacing (tracking-widest)
- Prices: Inter medium weight

## Spacing
- Generous whitespace throughout
- Section padding: py-20 lg:py-28
- Container max-width: max-w-7xl

## Components
- Buttons: Gold (#C8A96E) solid or outlined with gold border
- Dividers: Thin hairline, gradient gold or solid light
- Cards: Warm sand/pearl backgrounds, subtle shadows
- Hover: Smooth transitions (0.3s ease), subtle scale or shadow
- Nav: Transparent over hero, solid cream on scroll

## Imagery
- Warm-lit gold jewelry on dark/neutral backgrounds
- Editorial-style photography
- Use data-strk-img for stock images
