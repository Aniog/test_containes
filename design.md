# Velmora Fine Jewelry Design System

## Brand direction
Velmora uses a warm editorial luxury aesthetic: soft ivory surfaces, deep espresso text, and restrained champagne-gold accents. The mood is quiet, elevated, and intimate rather than loud or promotional.

## Color system
Use named Tailwind colors from `tailwind.config.js` only.

- `ivory`: main page background for softness and premium whitespace
- `shell`: secondary cards and panels
- `mist`: hairline borders and dividers
- `ink`: primary text and high-contrast UI surfaces
- `espresso`: hero overlays and dark editorial blocks
- `truffle`: muted body text and secondary navigation
- `champagne`: primary accent for buttons, highlights, and key details
- `brass`: darker metallic accent for outlines and hover states

## Typography
- Headings, logo, and product names: Cormorant Garamond (`font-display`)
- Body, navigation, filters, and UI: Inter (`font-sans`)
- Product names should be uppercase with `tracking-luxury`

## Spacing and layout
- Generous whitespace with strong vertical rhythm
- Thin dividers and understated borders using `mist`
- Use asymmetrical editorial layouts on desktop and clean stacking on mobile
- Cards should breathe, with soft shadows instead of heavy borders

## Components
- Buttons should feel premium: either solid `champagne` with dark text or transparent with fine `mist` border
- Navigation should use subtle blur and refined transitions
- Product cards should prioritize imagery, uppercase names, and restrained metadata
- Accent blocks should stay readable with explicit foreground colors

## Do
- Keep imagery warm, elegant, and close to skin tones or sculptural jewelry still life
- Use hover transitions sparingly and smoothly
- Maintain strong text contrast on every background

## Don't
- Do not use discount-style badges, loud color pops, or aggressive gradients
- Do not mix multiple palette directions
- Do not use invisible or low-contrast text on tinted surfaces
