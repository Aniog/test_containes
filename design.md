# SSourcing China - Design System

## Brand Identity
Professional B2B sourcing agent website. Trustworthy, international, clean, corporate.

## Color Palette
- Primary: #1e3a5f (dark navy blue - trust, professionalism)
- Primary Light: #2d5a8a
- Accent: #c9922b (gold - quality, premium)
- Accent Light: #e8b84b
- Background: #ffffff
- Surface: #f5f7fa (light gray-blue)
- Text Primary: #1a1a2e
- Text Secondary: #5a6a7e
- Border: #dce3eb
- Success: #2d8a4e

## Typography
- Font: Inter (Google Fonts)
- Headings: font-bold, tracking-tight
- H1: text-4xl md:text-5xl lg:text-6xl
- H2: text-3xl md:text-4xl
- H3: text-xl md:text-2xl
- Body: text-base text-gray-600
- Small: text-sm text-gray-500

## Spacing
- Sections: py-16 md:py-24
- Container: max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8
- Card padding: p-6 md:p-8

## Components
- Cards: bg-white rounded-lg shadow-sm border border-gray-100
- Buttons Primary: bg-[#1e3a5f] hover:bg-[#2d5a8a] text-white font-semibold rounded-lg px-6 py-3 transition-colors
- Buttons Accent: bg-[#c9922b] hover:bg-[#e8b84b] text-white font-semibold rounded-lg px-6 py-3 transition-colors

## Visual Style
- Clean, lots of white space
- Subtle shadows and borders
- Professional photography style (factory, warehouse, QC, shipping)
- Icons: Lucide React
- No exaggerated gradients or animations
- Trust signals: certifications, numbers, testimonials

## Do's
- Use navy/white/gold color scheme consistently
- Keep sections well-spaced with clear hierarchy
- Use professional, factual language
- Show real numbers and statistics

## Don'ts
- No bright/neon colors
- No exaggerated claims
- No stock photos that look fake
- No heavy animations or parallax
