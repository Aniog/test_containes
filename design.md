# SSourcing China — Design System

## Brand Identity
Professional, trustworthy, international B2B sourcing company based in China.
Tone: clear, practical, no exaggerated claims.

## Color Palette
- **Navy** `#0D2B4E` — primary dark, headings, navbar bg → `text-brand-navy`, `bg-brand-navy`
- **Blue** `#1A5FA8` — primary brand blue, links, buttons → `text-brand-blue`, `bg-brand-blue`
- **Sky** `#2E8BC0` — secondary accent, hover states → `text-brand-sky`
- **Accent** `#E8A020` — CTA buttons, highlights, badges → `bg-brand-accent`, `text-brand-accent`
- **Light** `#F0F6FF` — section backgrounds, cards → `bg-brand-light`
- **Muted** `#6B7A8D` — body text, subtitles → `text-brand-muted`
- **Border** `#D1DCE8` — dividers, card borders → `border-brand-border`

## Typography
- **Display font**: Poppins (headings, hero) → `font-display`
- **Body font**: Inter (body, UI) → `font-sans`
- H1: `font-display text-4xl md:text-5xl lg:text-6xl font-bold text-brand-navy`
- H2: `font-display text-3xl md:text-4xl font-bold text-brand-navy`
- H3: `font-display text-xl font-semibold text-brand-navy`
- Body: `font-sans text-base text-gray-700`
- Muted: `font-sans text-sm text-brand-muted`

## Spacing
- Section padding: `py-16 md:py-24`
- Container: `max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8`
- Card padding: `p-6 md:p-8`
- Gap between cards: `gap-6 md:gap-8`

## Buttons
- Primary CTA: `bg-brand-accent hover:bg-amber-500 text-white font-semibold px-6 py-3 rounded-lg transition-colors`
- Secondary: `bg-brand-blue hover:bg-brand-sky text-white font-semibold px-6 py-3 rounded-lg transition-colors`
- Outline: `border-2 border-brand-blue text-brand-blue hover:bg-brand-blue hover:text-white px-6 py-3 rounded-lg transition-colors`

## Cards
- `bg-white rounded-xl shadow-sm border border-brand-border p-6 hover:shadow-md transition-shadow`

## Navbar
- Background: `bg-brand-navy`
- Logo text: white, Poppins bold
- Nav links: `text-gray-300 hover:text-white`
- CTA button: `bg-brand-accent text-white`

## Sections
- White sections: `bg-white`
- Light sections: `bg-brand-light`
- Dark sections: `bg-brand-navy text-white`

## Do's
- Use Poppins for all headings
- Use Inter for body text
- Keep generous whitespace
- Use brand-accent for primary CTAs
- Use trust signals (numbers, certifications, client logos)

## Don'ts
- No exaggerated claims ("best in the world", "guaranteed")
- No low-contrast text on colored backgrounds
- No cluttered layouts
- No generic stock photo placeholders without context
