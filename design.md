# Visual Design Guidelines - SSourcing China

## Core Principles
- **Clean & Trustworthy**: Professional B2B look with high contrast and clear typography.
- **International B2B**: Modern corporate aesthetic that appeals to global buyers.
- **Vibrant CTAs**: Use orange for primary actions to stand out against corporate blue.

## Typography
- **Font Family**: Inter, sans-serif (Google Fonts)
- **Headings**: Semibold/Bold (600/700), Line-height: 1.2
- **Body**: Regular (400), Line-height: 1.5, Size: 16px

## Colors
- **Primary (Corporate Blue)**: `text-blue-900`, `bg-blue-900` (#1e3a8a)
- **Secondary (Action Orange)**: `text-orange-600`, `bg-orange-600` (#ea580c)
- **Neutral (Slate)**: `text-slate-600`, `bg-slate-50`
- **Background**: `bg-white`
- **Border/Divider**: `border-slate-200`

## Components & Styles
- **Buttons**: Rounded (8px), padding (12px 24px), transition-all.
- **Cards**: Background white, border 1px slate-200, subtle shadow (shadow-sm).
- **Sections**: Vertical padding (py-16 or py-24), alternating backgrounds (white and slate-50).

## Do's
- Use realistic imagery related to factories, quality control, and shipping.
- Maintain consistent white space between sections.
- Ensure all text is highly readable against its background.

## Don'ts
- Don't use exaggerated marketing claims.
- Don't use overly bright or neon colors other than the action orange.
- Don't crowd too many elements in one section.
