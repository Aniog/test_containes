# Visual Design Guidelines - SSourcing China

## Colors
- **Primary:** #003366 (Trust Blue) - Tailwind: `bg-primary` or `text-primary`
- **Accent:** #eab308 (Industrial Yellow) - Tailwind: `bg-accent` or `text-accent`
- **Secondary:** #f4f4f4 (Light Gray) - Tailwind: `bg-secondary`
- **Text Primary:** #1f2937 (Slate 800)
- **Text Muted:** #6b7280 (Slate 500)

## Typography
- **Headings:** Inter, Bold (700)
- **Body:** Inter, Regular (400)
- **CTA Buttons:** Bold, Uppercase, Primary/Accent background

## Spacing & Borders
- **Section Padding:** `py-16` or `py-24`
- **Container Max Width:** `max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4`
- **Rounded Corners:** `rounded-lg` (8px)
- **Borders:** `border border-slate-200`

## Components
- **Cards:** White background, light shadow, subtle border.
- **Form Inputs:** Slate border, focuses to Primary Blue.
- **Badges:** Success (Green), Warning (Yellow).

## Do's
- Use high-quality factory and inspection images.
- Keep layout clean and breathable.
- Focus on clarity and professional tone.

## Don'ts
- Use overly bright or neon colors.
- Use low-contrast text.
- Use exaggerated marketing claims.
