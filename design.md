# SSourcing China - Design Guidelines

## Typography
- Font: Inter (Google Fonts), weights 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800
- Headings: font-bold, tracking-tight
- Body: font-normal, text-base leading-relaxed
- Small / captions: text-sm, text-gray-500

## Colors
- Primary Blue: #1E3A5F (deep navy - trust, professionalism)
- Primary Blue Light: #2C5F8A
- Accent Orange: #E87722 (CTAs, highlights)
- Accent Orange Hover: #C85D18
- White: #FFFFFF
- Off-white Background: #F7F8FA
- Light Gray: #F0F2F5
- Medium Gray: #D1D5DB
- Text Dark: #111827
- Text Medium: #4B5563
- Text Light: #6B7280
- Success Green: #059669
- Border Light: #E5E7EB

## Spacing
- Section padding: py-16 md:py-24
- Container: max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8
- Card padding: p-6 md:p-8
- Gap between cards: gap-8

## Visual Style
- Clean, professional B2B look
- White backgrounds with subtle gray sections for contrast
- Blue accent elements for trust
- Orange only for primary CTAs
- Subtle shadows on cards (shadow-sm, shadow-md)
- Rounded corners: rounded-lg for cards, rounded-md for buttons
- No excessive gradients or decorative elements
- Realistic photography through stock image system

## Do's
- Use white space generously
- Keep text concise and professional
- Use numbered steps for processes
- Show trust markers (years, clients, certifications)

## Don'ts
- No exaggerated claims or hype language
- No flashy animations
- No dark mode sections
- No emoji in professional content
