# SSourcing China - Design System

## Brand Identity
- Professional B2B sourcing agent website
- Clean, trustworthy, international feel
- Target: overseas buyers looking for China sourcing support

## Colors
- **Primary**: `navy` (#1e3a5f) - trust, professionalism
- **Primary Light**: `navy-light` (#2a4f7f) - hover states
- **Accent**: `orange` (#e86c2e) - CTAs, highlights, energy
- **Accent Hover**: `orange-dark` (#d05a1e) - button hover
- **Background**: `slate-50` - light, clean page background
- **Surface**: `white` - cards, sections
- **Text Primary**: `slate-900` - headings, body
- **Text Secondary**: `slate-600` - descriptions, muted text
- **Text Light**: `white` - on dark backgrounds
- **Border**: `slate-200` - subtle borders
- **Success**: `emerald-600` - trust indicators

## Typography
- **Font Family**: Inter (Google Fonts)
- **Headings**: font-bold, tracking-tight
  - H1: text-4xl md:text-5xl lg:text-6xl
  - H2: text-3xl md:text-4xl
  - H3: text-xl md:text-2xl
  - H4: text-lg font-semibold
- **Body**: text-base text-slate-600 leading-relaxed
- **Small**: text-sm text-slate-500

## Spacing
- Section padding: py-16 md:py-24
- Container: max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8
- Card padding: p-6 md:p-8
- Gap between cards: gap-6 md:gap-8

## Components

### Buttons
- Primary CTA: bg-orange text-white px-6 py-3 rounded-lg font-semibold hover:bg-orange-dark transition-colors
- Secondary: border-2 border-navy text-navy px-6 py-3 rounded-lg font-semibold hover:bg-navy hover:text-white transition-colors
- Ghost: text-navy hover:text-navy-light underline

### Cards
- bg-white rounded-xl shadow-sm border border-slate-200 p-6 hover:shadow-md transition-shadow
- No excessive shadows or borders

### Navigation
- bg-white border-b border-slate-200 sticky top-0 z-50
- Logo: font-bold text-xl text-navy
- Links: text-slate-700 hover:text-navy font-medium

### Sections
- Alternating white and slate-50 backgrounds
- Dark sections: bg-navy text-white for emphasis

## Do's
- Use plenty of whitespace
- Keep text concise and scannable
- Use icons (Lucide) to support text
- Use stock images for factory/QC/shipping contexts
- Maintain consistent spacing throughout

## Don'ts
- No gradients or flashy effects
- No dark mode (B2B audience prefers light)
- No rounded-full on cards
- No excessive animations
- No placeholder "Lorem ipsum" text
- No exaggerated marketing claims
