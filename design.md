# Visual identity - Velmora Fine Jewelry

## Mood
Quiet luxury, warm, editorial. Premium demi-fine jewelry. Not loud, not discount-looking.

## Color Palette
- **Primary (Background)**: Deep Refined Base - `#121212` (Rich Charcoal/Black)
- **Secondary (Accents)**: Warm Metallic Gold - `#C5A059`
- **Surface**: Soft Neutral - `#1C1C1C`
- **Text (Heading)**: Off-white/Ivory - `#F9F7F2`
- **Text (Body)**: Muted Ivory - `#D1D1D1`
- **Muted**: Dark Grey - `#2A2A2A`

## Typography
- **Headings**: `Cormorant Garamond`, serif. Elegant, large spacing for uppercase titles.
- **Body & UI**: `Inter`, sans-serif. Clean, readable.
- **Product Names**: Uppercase with wide letter-spacing (`tracking-[0.2em]`).

## Layout & Spacing
- Generous whitespace.
- Thin hairline dividers (`border-white/10`).
- Full-bleed editorial imagery.
- Soft shadows and subtle hover transitions.

## Components
- **Buttons**:
    - Solid: `bg-[#C5A059] text-[#121212] hover:bg-[#B38D48] transition-colors`
    - Outlined: `border border-[#C5A059] text-[#C5A059] hover:bg-[#C5A059] hover:text-[#121212] transition-all`
- **Cards**: Minimalist, image-focused, subtle hover elevation.
