# Design Guidelines for SSourcing China

## Visual Style
Clean, trustworthy, international, and professional B2B aesthetic.

## Colors
- **Primary**: Navy Blue (#0f172a / slate-900) - Represents stability and professionalism.
- **Secondary**: Professional Blue (#3b82f6 / blue-500) - For accents and CTAs.
- **Background**: Soft Gray (#f8fafc / slate-50) and White (#ffffff).
- **Text**: Deep Slate (#1e293b / slate-800) for high readability.
- **Accent**: Emerald Green (#10b981 / emerald-500) for success points and quality trust.

## Typography
- **Font Family**: 'Inter', Sans-serif
- **Headings**: Semi-bold to Bold, tracking-tight
- **Body**: Regular, high line-height (1.6)

## Components
- **Buttons**: Rounded-md, primary color for main CTA, outline for secondary.
- **Cards**: Subtle borders, light shadows, ample white space.
- **Sections**: Clearly defined with white and light gray backgrounds.

## Do's
- Use realistic, high-quality images of factories, quality control, and shipping.
- Maintain consistent spacing (padding/margin).
- Focus on clarity and ease of navigation.
- Use iconography to break up text.

## Don'ts
- Avoid cluttered layouts.
- Don't use exaggerated claims or loud, distracting colors.
- No low-contrast text.
