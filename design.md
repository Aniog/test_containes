# SSourcing China - Design Style Guide

## Visual Identity
- **Brand**: Professional, trustworthy, international B2B
- **Primary Color**: Brand Blue (#1e40af) - represents trust and reliability
- **Secondary Color**: Accent Orange (#f97316) - represents action and urgency
- **Neutral Colors**: Slate scale (#0f172a to #f8fafc) for text and backgrounds

## Typography
- **Font Family**: Inter (Google Fonts)
- **Headings**: Bold, dark slate (#0f172a)
  - H1: 3rem/3.5rem (48/56px) - Hero headlines
  - H2: 2.25rem (36px) - Section titles
  - H3: 1.5rem (24px) - Subsection titles
- **Body**: Regular weight, medium slate (#475569)
  - Base: 1rem (16px)
  - Large: 1.125rem (18px)

## Colors
- **Primary Actions**: bg-brand-500 (#1e40af), hover:bg-brand-600 (#1e3a8a)
- **Secondary Actions**: bg-accent-500 (#f97316), hover:bg-accent-600 (#ea580c)
- **Backgrounds**: 
  - Primary: white (#ffffff)
  - Secondary: slate-50 (#f8fafc)
  - Tertiary: slate-100 (#f1f5f9)
- **Text**:
  - Primary: slate-900 (#0f172a)
  - Secondary: slate-600 (#475569)
  - Muted: slate-400 (#94a3b8)
  - On dark: white (#ffffff)

## Spacing
- **Section Padding**: py-16 md:py-24 (64px/96px)
- **Container**: max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8
- **Card Padding**: p-6 md:p-8 (24px/32px)
- **Gap**: gap-6 md:gap-8 (24px/32px)

## Border & Radius
- **Cards**: rounded-xl (12px)
- **Buttons**: rounded-lg (8px)
- **Images**: rounded-lg (8px)
- **Shadows**: shadow-sm for subtle, shadow-lg for emphasis

## Components
- **Buttons**: 
  - Primary: bg-brand-500 text-white px-6 py-3 rounded-lg font-semibold
  - Secondary: bg-accent-500 text-white px-6 py-3 rounded-lg font-semibold
  - Outline: border-2 border-brand-500 text-brand-500 px-6 py-3 rounded-lg
- **Cards**: bg-white rounded-xl shadow-sm p-6 border border-slate-200
- **Section Headers**: text-center with subtitle and decorative element

## Visual Style
- Clean, minimal design with generous whitespace
- Professional imagery (factories, QC, shipping)
- Trust badges and statistics prominently displayed
- Clear visual hierarchy with consistent spacing
- Mobile-first responsive design
