# Design Guidelines - SSourcing China

## Visual Style
- **Core Values**: Professional, Trustworthy, Efficient, International.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Primary: Midnight Blue (#002D62) - Representing stability and depth.
  - Secondary: Slate Gray (#F5F7FA) - For background and neutral spaces.
  - Accent: Vibrant Orange (#FF6B00) - For Call-to-Actions (CTAs) to drive inquiries.
  - Success: Emerald Green (#10B981) - For trust indicators and positive states.
- **Typography**:
  - Headings: 'Inter', Sans-serif, Bold (700) for hierarchy.
  - Body: 'Inter', Sans-serif, Regular (400) for readability.
- **Borders & Shadows**:
  - Subtle rounded corners (rounded-lg) for cards.
  - Light shadows (shadow-sm) for depth without clutter.
- **Spacing**:
  - Generous padding and margins to maintain a clean B2B look.

## Do's
- Use high-quality, realistic industry images (factories, QC inspections, shipping containers).
- Maintain consistent alignment across all sections.
- Use explicit foreground/background colors for readability.
- Add white space to emphasize important information.

## Don'ts
- Use overly bright or neon colors and fonts.
- Overcrowd sections with too much text.
- Use low-contrast text on colored backgrounds.
- Use exaggerated claims or "get rich quick" vibes.
