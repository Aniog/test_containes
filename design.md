# Velmora Fine Jewelry — Design System

## Brand Identity
Quiet luxury demi-fine jewelry. Premium-but-accessible. Editorial, warm, refined. NOT loud, NOT discount-looking.

## Color Palette
- Base: brand-ivory (#FEFCF9) — page background
- Surface: brand-cream (#FBF7F4), brand-blush (#F5EEE8), brand-sand (#EDE4DB)
- Accent: brand-gold (#C8A97E), brand-gold-dark (#B8935A), brand-gold-light (#DFCFB0)
- Text: brand-ink (#1A1A1A), brand-charcoal (#2C2C2C), brand-warmgray (#8B8580)

Rules:
- Gold is the ONLY accent color. No blue, no green, no purple.
- Gold buttons on light backgrounds. Gold text on dark/neutral surfaces.
- Never use gold text on white/cream — insufficient contrast. Use charcoal or ink.
- White/cream text on gold backgrounds is always readable.
- Warm gray (#8B8580) is for secondary text, labels, captions only. Not for primary content.

## Typography
- Headings / product names: Cormorant Garamond (serif)
- Body / UI / buttons: Inter (sans-serif)
- Product names: UPPERCASE with letter-spacing: 0.12em–0.25em
- Hero headline: 4xl–6xl serif, font-light or font-normal
- Section titles: 2xl–3xl serif
- Body: text-sm (0.875rem) to text-base (1rem), leading-relaxed

## Spacing
- Generous whitespace throughout
- Section padding: px-6 md:px-12 lg:px-16
- Section vertical padding: py-16 md:py-24
- Card gaps: gap-6 or gap-8
- Thin hairline dividers: border-t border-brand-sand/70

## Components
- Buttons: uppercase, letter-spacing wider, text-xs, Inter medium
  - Primary: bg-brand-gold text-white, hover bg-brand-gold-dark
  - Outline: border-brand-gold text-brand-gold, hover fills gold
  - Ghost: text-brand-charcoal, hover text-brand-gold
- Cards: bg-white, subtle shadow-sm, border border-brand-sand/50
- Navigation: transparent over hero, bg-white/95 backdrop-blur on scroll

## Imagery
- Warm-lit gold jewelry on dark/neutral backgrounds
- Editorial, model-worn when possible
- Large hero images (full-bleed)

## Animations
- Subtle fade-ins on scroll
- Smooth 300ms transitions on hover
- No jarring or bouncy animations
