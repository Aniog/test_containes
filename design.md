# SSourcing China Design System

## Brand Style
Clean, trustworthy, international B2B design for a China-based sourcing agent. The website should feel practical, structured, and credible for overseas buyers.

## Colors
- Primary navy: `brand-navy` (#0F2A43) for headers, navigation, high-emphasis text, and footer backgrounds.
- Action blue: `brand-blue` (#1D6FA5) for CTAs, active navigation, links, and key icons.
- Warm accent: `brand-gold` (#C8872A) for small highlights, labels, and trust markers.
- Soft background: `brand-mist` (#F4F7FA) for alternating sections.
- Border gray: `brand-border` (#D8E1EA) for cards, forms, and dividers.
- Text charcoal: `brand-ink` (#152536) for readable body copy.
- Muted text: `brand-muted` (#5F7184) for secondary descriptions.

## Typography
Use Inter from Google Fonts. Headings should be bold, tight, and professional. Body text should be clear and practical with generous line-height.

## Layout
Use wide, structured sections with `max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8`. Desktop layouts should use multi-column grids. Mobile layouts should stack cleanly with comfortable spacing.

## Components
- Cards: white background, subtle border, soft shadow, rounded-2xl, clear text colors.
- Buttons: primary filled blue with white text; secondary white with navy text and border.
- Forms: white card, explicit labels, readable placeholders, strong focus states.
- Navigation: sticky, white, subtle shadow/border, clear active and hover states.

## Visuals
Use realistic sourcing-related stock imagery through the Strikingly image helper: factories, QC inspection, container shipping, production lines, product samples, and business coordination.

## Do
- Keep claims practical and specific.
- Make CTAs visible and repeated.
- Use high-contrast text on every surface.
- Use realistic B2B language.

## Don't
- Do not use exaggerated claims like “guaranteed best price”.
- Do not use low-contrast text.
- Do not make desktop layouts look like mobile stacks.
- Do not hardcode arbitrary colors in JSX; use Tailwind theme names.
