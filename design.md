# SSourcing China Design System

## Overview
Professional, clean, and trustworthy B2B design for a China-based sourcing agent website. The design should convey reliability, international business standards, and practical expertise.

## Typography
- **Primary Font**: Inter (Google Fonts)
- **Headings**: Bold weights (700-800), tight line-height
- **Body**: Regular weight (400-500), comfortable line-height (1.6-1.7)
- **Font Sizes**:
  - Hero title: 3.5rem - 5rem (responsive)
  - Section titles: 2rem - 3rem
  - Body text: 1rem - 1.125rem
  - Small text: 0.875rem

## Color Palette
- **Primary**: Slate-900 (#0f172a) - Headings, primary buttons, key accents
- **Secondary**: Slate-600 (#475569) - Body text, secondary elements
- **Accent**: Slate-100 (#f1f5f9) - Light backgrounds, subtle highlights
- **Background**: White (#ffffff) and Slate-50 (#f8fafc) alternating sections
- **Text on dark**: White (#ffffff) and Slate-300 (#cbd5e1)
- **Success**: Green-500 (#22c55e) for checkmarks and success states
- **Warning**: Amber-400 (#fbbf24) for ratings
- **Error**: Red-500 (#ef4444) for error states

## Spacing
- **Section padding**: py-20 to py-32 (5rem - 8rem)
- **Container max-width**: max-w-7xl (1280px)
- **Grid gaps**: gap-8 to gap-12
- **Card padding**: p-8 to p-10

## Components
- **Buttons**: Rounded-lg (0.5rem), solid backgrounds, hover states
- **Cards**: Rounded-xl (0.75rem), subtle borders, hover shadows
- **Forms**: Clean inputs with focus rings, proper labels
- **Navigation**: Fixed header with backdrop blur on scroll

## Visual Style
- Clean, minimal design with plenty of white space
- Subtle shadows and borders for depth
- Professional iconography (Lucide React)
- High contrast for readability
- Responsive design with mobile-first approach

## Do's
- Use consistent spacing and alignment
- Maintain high contrast for text readability
- Use subtle animations and transitions
- Keep the design clean and uncluttered
- Use professional, trustworthy imagery

## Don'ts
- Avoid overly bright or flashy colors
- Don't use low-contrast text
- Avoid cluttered layouts
- Don't use exaggerated claims or hype language
- Avoid inconsistent spacing
