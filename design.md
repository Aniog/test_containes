# Velmora Fine Jewelry — Design System

## Visual Identity
- Mood: quiet luxury, warm, editorial. Premium demi-fine jewelry.
- NOT loud, NOT discount-looking, NOT generic e-commerce.

## Color Palette
- Background: `#FAF8F5` (warm ivory) — primary page background
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` (pure white) — cards, overlays
- Text Primary: `#1A1A1A` (near black) — headings, body
- Text Secondary: `#6B6560` (warm taupe) — subtitles, muted text
- Accent: `#B8860B` (dark goldenrod) — CTAs, links, highlights
- Accent Hover: `#9A7209` (darker gold)
- Border: `#E8E2DA` (warm light beige) — dividers, card borders
- Dark: `#2C2420` (deep warm brown) — footer, dark sections
- Star: `#C9A96E` (soft gold) — ratings

## Typography
- Headings / Product Names: Cormorant Garamond (serif), elegant, uppercase with wide letter-spacing
- Body / UI: Inter (sans-serif), clean, readable
- Product names: UPPERCASE, tracking-widest (0.15em–0.2em)

## Spacing
- Generous whitespace throughout
- Section padding: py-16 to py-24 on desktop
- Card gaps: gap-6 to gap-8

## Borders & Dividers
- Thin hairline dividers: border border-[#E8E2DA]
- Subtle rounded corners: rounded-sm (2px) for cards, rounded-full for pills

## Shadows
- Soft, warm shadows: shadow-sm for cards, shadow-md for hover states
- No harsh shadows

## Buttons
- Primary: solid accent background (#B8860B), white text, uppercase, tracking-wide
- Secondary: outlined accent, transparent bg, accent border
- Hover: slight darken, smooth transition (duration-300)

## Animations
- Subtle hover transitions: duration-300, ease-out
- Fade-in on scroll (optional)
- Smooth cart drawer slide-in

## Do's
- Use generous whitespace
- Keep text readable with strong contrast
- Use serif for headings, sans-serif for body
- Warm, editorial imagery
- Restrained accent color usage

## Don'ts
- No loud colors or gradients
- No discount badges or sale-looking elements
- No generic e-commerce patterns
- No heavy shadows or borders
- No cluttered layouts
