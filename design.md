# SSourcing China - Design System

## Brand Identity
Professional China sourcing agent for global B2B buyers. Trustworthy, international, practical.

## Colors
- Primary Navy: #0f1d3d (headings, footer, dark sections)
- Accent Blue: #1a56db (CTAs, links, highlights)
- Light Blue: #3b82f6 (hover states)
- Accent Red: #c1292e (limited use, emphasis)
- Gold: #d4a853 (badges, trust markers, subtle highlights)
- White: #ffffff (backgrounds, cards)
- Slate surfaces: #f8fafc, #f1f5f9 (section alternation)

## Typography
- Font: Inter (Google Fonts, loaded in index.html)
- Headings: font-bold, tracking-tight
- Body: text-base leading-relaxed
- Small/caption: text-sm

## Layout
- Max container width: max-w-7xl (1280px)
- Section padding: py-20 md:py-28
- Card padding: p-6 md:p-8
- Card border radius: rounded-xl
- Button border radius: rounded-lg

## Spacing
- Section gaps: 20-28 (py)
- Content gaps: 12-16
- Grid gaps: 8-12

## Do's
- Use white cards on light gray backgrounds for contrast
- Use navy text on light backgrounds
- Use white text on navy/dark backgrounds
- Keep CTAs in brand blue with white text
- Use gold sparingly for trust badges
- Maintain generous whitespace

## Don'ts
- No dark text on dark backgrounds
- No light text on light backgrounds  
- No exaggerated marketing claims
- No neon or overly bright colors
- No cluttered layouts
