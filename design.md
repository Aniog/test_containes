# SSourcing China - Design System

## Visual Style
Clean, trustworthy, international B2B aesthetic. Professional color palette with navy blue (#0F2942) as primary, slate gray (#475569) for text, and light backgrounds (#F8FAFC, #FFFFFF). Accent color is teal (#0D9488) for CTAs and highlights.

## Typography
- Font: Inter (Google Fonts)
- Headings: 600-700 weight, tight tracking
- Body: 400-500 weight, 1.6 line-height
- Sizes: H1 48px, H2 32px, H3 24px, Body 16px

## Colors
- Primary: #0F2942 (deep navy)
- Secondary: #475569 (slate)
- Accent: #0D9488 (teal)
- Background: #F8FAFC, #FFFFFF
- Borders: #E2E8F0
- Success: #059669

## Layout
- Max width: 1280px
- Section padding: 80px vertical on desktop, 48px on mobile
- Consistent 24px grid gaps
- Responsive: mobile-first with md: and lg: breakpoints

## Components
- Buttons: rounded-lg, 600 weight, teal primary, navy secondary
- Cards: white bg, subtle shadow, 8px border radius
- Forms: clean inputs with slate borders, teal focus states
- Navigation: fixed top bar, clean links, mobile hamburger

## Do's
- Use ample white space
- Keep text concise and practical
- Show realistic factory/QC/shipping imagery
- Maintain professional tone throughout

## Don'ts
- Avoid bright or playful colors
- No exaggerated claims or hype language
- Don't overcrowd sections
- Avoid decorative elements that feel consumer-facing
