# SSourcing China — Design System

## Brand Overview
A China-based B2B sourcing agent for overseas buyers. The site must feel clean, trustworthy, and international. No hype, no exaggerated claims. Practical and professional.

## Color Palette
- **Primary**: `#0f4c81` — strong, stable navy blue (trust, B2B, international)
- **Primary Accent**: `#e6a23c` — warm gold/amber for CTAs and key highlights
- **Background**: `#ffffff` — clean white pages
- **Surface**: `#f6f8fb` — very light blue-gray for alternating sections
- **Surface Dark**: `#0b1d2e` — deep navy footer
- **Text Primary**: `#0f172a` — near-black slate for headings and body
- **Text Secondary**: `#475569` — slate for descriptions and supporting text
- **Text Muted**: `#64748b` — captions, meta data
- **Border**: `#e2e8f0` — light slate borders

## Typography
- **Font family**: `Inter, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`
- **Headings**: tight line-height (1.15–1.25), font-weight 700–800
- **Body**: line-height 1.65, font-weight 400
- **Hero headline**: `text-4xl md:text-5xl lg:text-6xl`
- **Section title**: `text-3xl md:text-4xl`
- **Section subtitle / body**: `text-base md:text-lg text-slate-600`

## Spacing
- Section vertical padding: `py-16 md:py-24 lg:py-28`
- Container max-width: `max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8`
- Card padding: `p-6 md:p-8`
- Grid gaps: `gap-6 md:gap-8`

## Components
- **Buttons**
  - Primary: `bg-amber-500 hover:bg-amber-600 text-slate-900 font-semibold rounded-lg px-6 py-3`
  - Secondary / outline: `border border-slate-300 text-slate-700 hover:border-slate-400 hover:bg-slate-50 rounded-lg px-5 py-2.5`
- **Cards**: `bg-white rounded-xl border border-slate-200 shadow-sm hover:shadow-md transition`
- **Badges**: `bg-blue-50 text-blue-800 text-sm font-medium px-3 py-1 rounded-full`
- **Section labels**: small uppercase tracking-wide text-blue-700 font-semibold

## Imagery
- Use stock-image tagging system (`data-strk-img` and `data-strk-bg`) for factory, QC, shipping, and product visuals.
- Avoid generic lifestyle images; prefer manufacturing, logistics, inspection, and business visuals.

## Do's
- Keep layouts spacious and breathable.
- Use the navy + amber pairing for CTAs to stand out.
- Alternate white and light-blue-gray section backgrounds for visual rhythm.
- Make trust signals visible: years of experience, verification steps, client logos, certifications.
- Use realistic, business-relevant visuals.

## Don'ts
- No bright neon colors, no gradients all over the page.
- No exaggerated superlatives ("best", "#1", "guaranteed cheapest").
- No single-column desktop layouts.
- No low-contrast text.
