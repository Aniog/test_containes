# Design Guidelines - Velmora Fine Jewelry

## Visual Identity
- **Mood**: Quiet luxury, warm, editorial, premium.
- **Brand Name**: VELMORA (Serif, Uppercase, wide letter-spacing).

## Color Palette
- **Primary (Base)**: `#1A1A1A` (Deep Charcoal for text and high-contrast elements)
- **Background**: `#F9F7F2` (Soft Cream/Bone for an editorial feel)
- **Accent (Metallic)**: `#C5A059` (Warm Gold for CTA buttons and highlights)
- **Secondary**: `#EAE6DF` (Light Stone for dividers and subtle backgrounds)
- **Muted**: `#707070` (Gray for secondary text)

## Typography
- **Headings**: `Cormorant Garamond`, serif. (Luxurious, elegant)
- **Body & UI**: `Inter`, sans-serif. (Clean, modern, readable)
- **Product Names**: `Cormorant Garamond`, serif, UPPERCASE, tracking-widest.

## Spacing & Layout
- **Whitespace**: Generous, providing "room to breathe".
- **Dividers**: Thin hairlines (`#EAE6DF`).
- **Cards**: Minimalist, no borders, soft shadows on interaction.

## UI Elements
- **Buttons**:
  - Primary: Solid `#C5A059` with white text.
  - Secondary: Outlined `#1A1A1A` or `#C5A059`.
- **Transitions**: Smooth (300ms), subtle ease-in-out.
- **Shadows**: Very soft, used sparingly for depth (e.g., cart drawer).

## Do's & Don'ts
- **Do**: Use high-quality editorial imagery.
- **Do**: Keep focus on the jewelry.
- **Do**: Use uppercase for product titles and navigation links.
- **Don't**: Use loud colors or heavy gradients.
- **Don't**: Overcrowd sections with too much text or products.
- **Don't**: Use generic sans-serif for headings.
