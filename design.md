# SSourcing China - Design System

## Typography
- Font family: Inter (Google Fonts)
- Headings: font-semibold to font-bold, tracking-tight
- Body: font-normal, leading-relaxed
- Small text: text-sm

## Colors
- Primary: blue-700 (#1D4ED8) - trust, professionalism
- Primary hover: blue-800
- Primary foreground: white
- Secondary: slate-50 to slate-100 backgrounds
- Accent: amber-500 for CTAs and highlights
- Text: slate-900 (headings), slate-600 (body), slate-500 (muted)
- Background: white, slate-50
- Card: white with subtle shadow
- Border: slate-200

## Spacing
- Section padding: py-16 md:py-20 lg:py-24
- Container: max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8
- Card padding: p-6
- Gap between items: gap-6 or gap-8

## Components
- Buttons: rounded-md, px-6 py-3 for primary CTAs
- Cards: rounded-lg, border, shadow-sm, hover:shadow-md
- Badges: rounded-full, small text
- Forms: rounded-md borders, focus rings

## Layout
- Max width: max-w-7xl for content
- Grid: grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-3 for cards
- Two-column: grid-cols-1 lg:grid-cols-2 for feature sections

## Visual Style
- Clean, professional B2B aesthetic
- Subtle shadows, not heavy
- Blue as primary brand color
- White space for readability
- Factory/QC/shipping imagery via stock image system
- No exaggerated claims, practical tone
