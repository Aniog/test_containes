# SSourcing China - Design System

## Brand Identity
- Professional B2B sourcing agent website
- Clean, trustworthy, international feel
- Target: overseas buyers looking for China sourcing support

## Colors
- **Primary**: `brand-navy` (#1B2B4B) - deep navy for trust and authority
- **Primary Accent**: `brand-blue` (#2563EB) - action blue for CTAs and links
- **Secondary**: `brand-gold` (#D4A853) - subtle gold for premium accents
- **Background**: `brand-light` (#F8FAFC) - clean off-white
- **Surface**: `brand-white` (#FFFFFF) - card backgrounds
- **Text Primary**: `brand-dark` (#1E293B) - near-black for body text
- **Text Secondary**: `brand-muted` (#64748B) - muted gray for secondary text
- **Border**: `brand-border` (#E2E8F0) - subtle borders
- **Success**: `brand-green` (#16A34A) - trust indicators

## Typography
- **Font**: Inter (Google Fonts)
- **Headings**: font-bold, tracking-tight
  - H1: text-4xl md:text-5xl lg:text-6xl
  - H2: text-3xl md:text-4xl
  - H3: text-xl md:text-2xl
  - H4: text-lg md:text-xl
- **Body**: text-base (16px), text-brand-muted for secondary
- **Small**: text-sm

## Spacing
- Section padding: py-16 md:py-24
- Container: max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8
- Card padding: p-6 md:p-8
- Gap between cards: gap-6 md:gap-8

## Components
- **Buttons**: 
  - Primary: bg-brand-blue text-white px-6 py-3 rounded-lg font-semibold hover:bg-blue-700 transition
  - Secondary: border-2 border-brand-navy text-brand-navy px-6 py-3 rounded-lg font-semibold hover:bg-brand-navy hover:text-white transition
- **Cards**: bg-white rounded-xl shadow-sm border border-brand-border p-6 hover:shadow-md transition
- **Section headings**: text-center with subtitle in text-brand-muted

## Do's
- Use generous whitespace
- Keep text concise and scannable
- Use icons (Lucide) to support text
- Use stock images for factory/QC/shipping contexts
- Maintain consistent section structure

## Don'ts
- No exaggerated claims or hype language
- No dark backgrounds for main content (only hero/footer)
- No overly decorative elements
- No more than 3 font weights per section
