# Design System: Velmora Fine Jewelry

## Mood & Spirit
- **Quiet Luxury**: Minimalist yet rich, focusing on quality and subtle details.
- **Warm & Editorial**: Lighting should feel soft and golden; layouts should feel like a high-end fashion magazine.
- **Accessible Premium**: High-end aesthetic that remains welcoming and clear.

## Color Palette
- **Primary (Background)**: `#FDFCFB` (Soft Cream) - `bg-[#FDFCFB]`
- **Secondary (Text/UI)**: `#1A1A1A` (Deep Charcoal) - `text-[#1A1A1A]`, `bg-[#1A1A1A]`
- **Accent (Gold)**: `#C5A059` (Muted Gold) - `text-[#C5A059]`, `border-[#C5A059]`, `bg-[#C5A059]`
- **Divider**: `#E5E5E5` (Thin Hairline) - `border-[#E5E5E5]`
- **Success**: `#2D5A27` (Deep Forest Green) - `text-[#2D5A27]`

## Typography
- **Headings & Product Names**: `Cormorant Garamond`, serif.
  - Class: `font-serif`
  - Style: Elegant, high contrast. Product names in UPPERCASE with `tracking-[0.2em]`.
- **Body & UI**: `Inter`, sans-serif.
  - Class: `font-sans`
  - Style: Clean, readable, professional.

## UI Components
- **Buttons**:
  - Primary: Solid Charcoal (`bg-[#1A1A1A] text-white`) or Muted Gold (`bg-[#C5A059] text-white`).
  - Secondary: Outlined (`border border-[#1A1A1A] text-[#1A1A1A]`).
- **Cards**: Minimalist, generous padding, subtle hover transitions (slight scale or opacity change).
- **Dividers**: 1px hairline (`h-[1px] bg-[#E5E5E5]`).

## Photography Style
- Warm, natural lighting.
- Jewelry on skin (model) or neutral/stone textures.
- Shallow depth of field for editorial feel.
