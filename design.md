# SSourcing China - Design System

## Brand Identity
- **Name**: SSourcing China
- **Tagline**: China Sourcing Agent for Global Buyers
- **Industry**: B2B Sourcing / Procurement
- **Tone**: Professional, clear, practical, trustworthy

## Color Palette
- **Primary**: #0F4C81 (Deep Trust Blue)
- **Primary Dark**: #0A3560
- **Accent**: #E76F51 (Warm Coral - for CTAs)
- **Accent Hover**: #D45A3D
- **Background**: #FFFFFF
- **Surface**: #F8FAFC
- **Surface Dark**: #F1F5F9
- **Text Primary**: #1E293B
- **Text Secondary**: #64748B
- **Text Muted**: #94A3B8
- **Border**: #E2E8F0
- **Success**: #10B981
- **Warning**: #F59E0B

## Typography
- **Font Family**: Inter (Google Fonts)
- **Headings**: font-weight 700-800, tight line-height (1.1-1.2)
- **Body**: font-weight 400-500, line-height 1.6-1.7
- **Hero Title**: text-4xl md:text-5xl lg:text-6xl
- **Section Title**: text-3xl md:text-4xl
- **Card Title**: text-xl
- **Body**: text-base
- **Small**: text-sm

## Spacing
- **Section Padding**: py-16 md:py-24
- **Container Max Width**: max-w-7xl
- **Container Padding**: px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8
- **Card Padding**: p-6 md:p-8
- **Gap**: gap-6 md:gap-8

## Component Styles

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: bg-[#E76F51] text-white px-8 py-3.5 rounded-lg font-semibold hover:bg-[#D45A3D] transition
- **Secondary**: bg-white text-[#0F4C81] border-2 border-[#0F4C81] px-8 py-3.5 rounded-lg font-semibold hover:bg-[#0F4C81] hover:text-white transition
- **Ghost**: bg-transparent text-[#0F4C81] hover:underline

### Cards
- Background: white
- Border: 1px solid #E2E8F0
- Border Radius: 12px
- Shadow: shadow-sm hover:shadow-md transition
- Padding: p-6 or p-8

### Navigation
- Fixed top, white background, border-bottom
- Links: text-[#1E293B] hover:text-[#0F4C81]
- CTA in nav: Primary button style, smaller

## Layout Rules
- Max container width: 1280px centered
- Consistent section vertical rhythm
- Grid layouts: 1 col mobile, 2 col tablet, 3-4 col desktop
- Generous whitespace between sections

## Image Strategy
- Use stock image system for factory, QC inspection, shipping, and product imagery
- Prefer realistic business/industrial photography
- Avoid overly generic stock photos

## Do's and Don'ts
- DO use plenty of whitespace
- DO keep text readable with strong contrast
- DO use the accent color sparingly (CTAs only)
- DON'T use more than 2 font sizes in a single component
- DON'T use dark backgrounds for content sections (keep light)
- DON'T make exaggerated claims - keep tone factual
