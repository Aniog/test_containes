# SSourcing China — Design System

A clean, trustworthy, international B2B website for a China-based sourcing agent.

## Brand Colors

- **Primary (Navy):** `#0f2a55` — used for headings, primary buttons, footer.
- **Primary Accent (Sky):** `#2563eb` — links, icons, highlights.
- **Trust Blue:** `#1e40af` — trust badges, section accents.
- **Surface White:** `#ffffff` — cards, page background.
- **Soft Gray:** `#f8fafc` — alternating sections.
- **Text Gray:** `#334155` — body copy.
- **Muted Text:** `#64748b` — captions, secondary text.
- **Border:** `#e2e8f0` — subtle dividers.
- **Destructive:** `#dc2626` — errors.

## Typography

- **Font:** Geist Variable, Inter, system-ui, sans-serif.
- **Headings:** Semi-bold to bold, tight line-height.
- **Body:** Regular weight, 1.6 line-height.
- **Hero Headline:** `text-4xl md:text-5xl lg:text-6xl font-bold`.

## Spacing & Radius

- **Section padding:** `py-16 md:py-24`.
- **Container:** `max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8`.
- **Card radius:** `rounded-lg`.
- **Button radius:** `rounded-md`.

## Components

- **Buttons:** Solid navy primary; white ghost/outline secondary.
- **Cards:** White background, subtle border, light shadow.
- **Badges:** Rounded-full, navy or gray.
- **Inputs:** Light border, rounded-md, focus ring in primary.
- **Accordion:** Card-style items with chevron rotation.

## Imagery

- Use realistic factory, QC inspection, and logistics visuals via `data-strk-img`.
- Avoid generic stock clichés; prefer action-oriented manufacturing scenes.

## Do's

- Keep layouts breathable with generous whitespace.
- Use navy + white for a professional B2B feel.
- Make CTAs clear and repeated.
- Ensure text contrast on all backgrounds.

## Don'ts

- Use exaggerated claims or aggressive sales language.
- Use low-contrast text on light or dark backgrounds.
- Crowd mobile layouts; stack cleanly on small screens.
