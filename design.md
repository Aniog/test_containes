# SSourcing China - B2B Website Design Guidelines

## Visual Style
Professional, clean, trustworthy, international B2B aesthetic. Use a restrained color palette with blues and grays to convey reliability and professionalism.

## Color Palette
- Primary: #1E3A5F (Deep Navy Blue) - Trust, professionalism
- Secondary: #2E5A8B (Medium Blue) - Accents and CTAs
- Accent: #4A90D9 (Light Blue) - Highlights
- Neutral Dark: #1F2937 (Slate Gray) - Text
- Neutral Light: #F3F4F6 (Light Gray) - Backgrounds
- White: #FFFFFF - Cards, sections
- Success: #059669 (Green) - Trust indicators

## Typography
- Font Family: Inter (system-ui fallback)
- Headings: 600-700 weight, tight tracking
- Body: 400-500 weight, 1.6 line-height
- Use semantic hierarchy: h1 (2.5rem), h2 (1.875rem), h3 (1.5rem)

## Layout & Spacing
- Max container width: 1280px
- Section padding: 4rem top/bottom (mobile: 3rem)
- Consistent 1.5rem gaps between cards
- Responsive: Mobile-first with md: (768px) and lg: (1024px) breakpoints

## Components
- Buttons: Rounded corners (0.375rem), clear hover states
  - Primary: Navy background, white text
  - Secondary: White background, navy border
- Cards: White background, subtle shadow, clean borders
- Forms: Clean inputs with focus states, clear labels
- Navigation: Fixed top bar, clear active states

## Images
- Use data-strk-img for realistic factory, QC inspection, shipping visuals
- Maintain professional, authentic appearance
- Proper aspect ratios for consistency

## Tone
Professional, clear, practical. No exaggerated claims. Focus on value and process transparency.

## Do's
- Use ample white space
- Maintain consistent alignment
- Ensure high contrast for readability
- Use icons from lucide-react for visual clarity
- Show realistic process steps

## Don'ts
- Avoid bright or flashy colors
- No exaggerated marketing language
- Avoid cluttered layouts
- No decorative elements that distract from content
