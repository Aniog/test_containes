# SSourcing China Design System

## Brand Identity
- **Primary Color**: Navy Blue (#1B365D) - Trust, professionalism, international business
- **Secondary Color**: Deep Red (#C41E3A) - China reference, urgency, CTAs
- **Accent Color**: Golden Yellow (#D4A843) - Quality, premium service
- **Neutral Dark**: Charcoal (#1A1A2E) - Text, headers
- **Neutral Medium**: Slate Gray (#64748B) - Body text, descriptions
- **Neutral Light**: Off-White (#F8FAFC) - Backgrounds, cards
- **Background**: Light Gray (#F1F5F9) - Page background

## Typography
- **Headings**: Inter, system-ui - Bold, professional, clean
- **Body**: Inter, system-ui - Regular weight, readable
- **Accent**: Inter, system-ui - Medium weight for CTAs and highlights

## Layout
- **Max Width**: 1280px for main content
- **Section Padding**: py-16 md:py-24
- **Card Padding**: p-6 md:p-8
- **Gap**: gap-6 for grids, gap-4 for lists

## Components
- **Buttons**: 
  - Primary: bg-red-600 text-white hover:bg-red-700
  - Secondary: bg-navy-900 text-white hover:bg-navy-800
  - Outline: border-2 border-navy-900 text-navy-900
- **Cards**: bg-white rounded-xl shadow-sm border border-gray-100
- **Sections**: bg-white or bg-gray-50 alternating

## Visual Style
- Clean, spacious layouts with clear hierarchy
- Professional imagery showing factories, QC, shipping
- Subtle shadows and rounded corners
- Clear CTAs with contrasting colors
- Trust badges and certifications prominently displayed
