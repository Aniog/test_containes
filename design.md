# SSourcing China - Design System

## Typography
- Font family: Inter (Google Fonts)
- Headings: Inter, font-bold, tracking-tight
- Body: Inter, text-base, leading-relaxed
- Heading sizes: h1 (text-4xl md:text-5xl lg:text-6xl), h2 (text-3xl md:text-4xl), h3 (text-xl md:text-2xl)

## Colors
- Primary Blue: brand-500 (#1a56db), brand-600 (#1e40af), brand-700 (#1e3a8a)
- Dark Navy: brand-900 (#0f1d36), brand-950 (#0a1427)
- Accent Gold: accent-400 (#f59e0b), accent-500 (#d97706)
- Background: white (#ffffff), gray-50 (#f8fafc), gray-100 (#f1f5f9)
- Text: slate-700 (#334155), slate-800 (#1e293b), brand-900 for headings
- Muted text: slate-500 (#64748b)

## Buttons
- Primary CTA: bg-brand-500 hover:bg-brand-600 text-white font-semibold py-3 px-8 rounded-lg transition-colors
- Secondary: border-2 border-brand-500 text-brand-600 hover:bg-brand-50 font-semibold py-3 px-8 rounded-lg transition-colors
- Ghost: text-slate-600 hover:text-brand-600 font-medium

## Cards
- bg-white border border-gray-200 rounded-xl shadow-sm hover:shadow-md transition-shadow p-6 md:p-8

## Sections
- Standard section padding: py-16 md:py-24
- Alternating backgrounds: white and gray-50
- Hero section: bg-brand-900 text-white

## Spacing
- Section gaps: py-16 md:py-24
- Container: max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8

## Visual Style
- Clean, professional, international B2B look
- Deep navy blue as primary brand color, conveying trust and reliability
- Gold accent for CTAs and highlights
- Ample white space for readability
- Realistic factory/QC/shipping visuals via stock images
- No exaggerated claims, practical tone
- Subtle icons from Lucide React to support messaging

## Do's
- Use brand navy for headings and key UI elements
- Use gold accent sparingly for CTAs and attention points
- Keep text left-aligned for readability
- Use cards with subtle shadows for service/feature blocks
- Maintain consistent section padding

## Don'ts
- No bright neon colors or gradients
- No centered long-form body text
- No stock image cliches (handshakes, globes)
- No large bold ALL CAPS text
- No dark mode
