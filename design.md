# Velmora Fine Jewelry — Design System

## Visual Direction
- **Mood**: Quiet luxury, warm, editorial. Premium demi-fine jewelry presentation.
- **Aesthetic**: Refined minimalism meets warm editorial elegance. Think Vogue editorial meets high-end boutique.

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors
- **Ivory Cream** `#FAF8F5` — Primary background, warmth base
- **Warm White** `#FFFFFF` — Card surfaces, clean areas
- **Soft Charcoal** `#2D2926` — Primary text, logo
- **Warm Taupe** `#8B7E74` — Secondary text, muted elements

### Accent Colors
- **Champagne Gold** `#C9A962` — Primary accent, CTAs, highlights
- **Rich Gold** `#B8943F` — Hover states, emphasis
- **Deep Bronze** `#8B6914` — Active states

### Supporting Colors
- **Blush Pink** `#F5EBE0` — Subtle backgrounds, cards
- **Warm Gray** `#E8E4E0` — Borders, dividers
- **Success** `#4A7C59` — Confirmations
- **Error** `#A63D40` — Error states

## Typography

### Headings & Display
- **Font Family**: Cormorant Garamond (serif)
- **Weights**: 400 (regular), 500 (medium), 600 (semibold)
- **Product Names**: UPPERCASE with letter-spacing: 0.15em
- **Section Headings**: 48-72px desktop, 32-40px mobile

### Body & UI
- **Font Family**: Inter (sans-serif)
- **Weights**: 300 (light), 400 (regular), 500 (medium), 600 (semibold)
- **Body Text**: 16px, line-height 1.6
- **UI Elements**: 14px, line-height 1.5

## Spacing System
- Base unit: 4px
- Scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128px
- Section padding: 80-120px vertical desktop, 48-64px mobile

## Component Styles

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Champagne gold background, soft charcoal text, 48px height, uppercase with letter-spacing
- **Secondary**: Transparent with 1px gold border, gold text
- **Hover**: Darken 10%, subtle lift shadow
- **Border radius**: 2px (minimal, refined)

### Cards
- **Background**: Warm white or ivory
- **Border**: 1px warm gray (hairline)
- **Shadow**: 0 2px 8px rgba(45, 41, 38, 0.06)
- **Hover**: Lift with enhanced shadow

### Forms
- **Input height**: 48px
- **Border**: 1px warm gray
- **Focus**: Champagne gold border
- **Border radius**: 2px

### Dividers
- **Style**: 1px warm gray, hairline
- **Accent dividers**: Champagne gold for emphasis

## Motion & Transitions
- **Duration**: 200-300ms
- **Easing**: ease-out
- **Hover effects**: Subtle scale (1.02), lift, opacity changes
- **Page transitions**: Fade in 300ms
- **Image hovers**: Crossfade between images

## Breakpoints
- **Mobile**: < 640px
- **Tablet**: 640px - 1024px
- **Desktop**: > 1024px

## Visual Assets
- **Images**: Warm-lit, editorial photography style
- **Placeholders**: Neutral dark backgrounds (#2D2926) with gold accent elements
- **Icons**: Lucide React, 1.5px stroke weight
