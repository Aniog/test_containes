# SSourcing China Visual Design Guide

## Brand position
SSourcing China should feel like a practical, reliable B2B partner for overseas buyers. The design must communicate trust, operational discipline, and clear sourcing expertise without exaggerated promises.

## Visual style
- Overall look: clean international B2B, structured, calm, and credible.
- Layout: wide desktop sections with strong alignment, generous spacing, and clear content hierarchy. Mobile should stack content naturally with comfortable spacing.
- Typography: Inter for all text. Use bold, compact headlines and readable body copy.
- Color direction: use Tailwind semantic palettes only. Primary navy/slate surfaces (`slate-950`, `slate-900`, `slate-800`) with blue accents (`blue-700`, `blue-600`, `sky-100`) and restrained amber highlights (`amber-400`, `amber-100`).
- Backgrounds: mostly white, slate-50, and soft blue/slate panels. Dark sections must explicitly use white or slate-100 text.
- Borders: subtle `border-slate-200` or dark translucent borders on navy surfaces. Cards should use rounded corners and light shadows.
- Imagery: realistic factory floors, quality control checks, shipping containers, logistics teams, and supplier meetings. Use image tags with SSourcing stock-image attributes, not hardcoded image URLs.

## Components
- Header: sticky, white, high contrast, clear navigation, visible CTA.
- Buttons: primary blue filled button for quote CTA; secondary white/slate outline buttons for supporting actions.
- Cards: white background, `text-slate-900`, rounded corners, border, and shadow where useful.
- Forms: white fields, clear dark labels, visible focus rings, practical B2B inquiry labels.
- Badges: readable foreground/background pairs such as blue-on-blue-50 or amber-on-amber-100.

## Do
- Keep copy professional, clear, and practical.
- Make the inquiry CTA visible in hero, navigation, services, and contact sections.
- Use realistic operational details: supplier verification, factory audits, QC checkpoints, production follow-up, shipping coordination.
- Ensure every text element is readable against its background.

## Don't
- Do not use exaggerated claims such as guaranteed lowest prices or risk-free sourcing.
- Do not use low contrast gray text on light backgrounds.
- Do not crowd mobile screens with too many columns.
- Do not use hardcoded external image URLs.
