# SSourcing China Design System

## Brand direction
- Clean, credible, international B2B presentation
- Practical sourcing partner tone, not promotional hype
- Visual story should feel operational: factory visits, quality inspection, supplier review, packaging, freight coordination

## Core palette
- Page background: `bg-slate-50`
- Primary surfaces: `bg-white`
- Dark hero / footer surfaces: `bg-slate-950`, `bg-slate-900`
- Primary text: `text-slate-950`
- Secondary text: `text-slate-600`
- Soft dividers: `border-slate-200`
- Brand accent: `sky-700` / `sky-800`
- Supporting accent: `amber-500`, `emerald-600`

## Typography
- Font family: Inter
- H1: `text-4xl md:text-6xl font-semibold tracking-tight`
- H2: `text-3xl md:text-4xl font-semibold tracking-tight`
- H3: `text-xl font-semibold`
- Body copy: `text-base md:text-lg leading-7 text-slate-600`
- Small labels / eyebrow: `text-sm font-semibold uppercase tracking-[0.2em]`

## Layout and spacing
- Main container: `mx-auto max-w-7xl px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8`
- Section spacing: `py-16 md:py-24`
- Card spacing: `p-6 md:p-8`
- Use generous whitespace and clear desktop layouts

## Components
- Navigation: white or slightly translucent surface with a clear CTA button
- Primary CTA: `bg-sky-700 text-white hover:bg-sky-800`
- Secondary CTA: `border border-slate-300 bg-white text-slate-900 hover:bg-slate-100`
- Cards: `rounded-3xl border border-slate-200 bg-white shadow-sm`
- Trust sections should use subtle tinted backgrounds instead of flashy gradients

## Imagery
- Use realistic factory, inspection, packaging, container loading, supplier meeting, and shipping visuals
- Avoid abstract tech visuals and generic lifestyle imagery
- Keep overlays dark enough so hero text stays clearly readable

## Do
- Keep all text highly legible on every surface
- Emphasize process clarity, operational control, and communication
- Use concise business language and buyer-centered headings

## Don't
- Do not use exaggerated claims or flashy marketing language
- Do not use low-contrast gray-on-gray text
- Do not clutter desktop layouts with narrow single-column sections
