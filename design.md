# SSourcing China — Design System

## Brand Identity
Professional, trustworthy, international B2B sourcing company based in China.

## Color Palette

### Primary — Deep Navy Blue
- Primary: `#1B3A6B` (navy-800) — main brand color, headers, CTAs
- Primary Light: `#2A5298` (navy-600) — hover states
- Primary Dark: `#0F2347` (navy-900) — dark backgrounds

### Accent — Warm Gold
- Accent: `#C8922A` (gold-600) — highlights, badges, icons
- Accent Light: `#E8B84B` (gold-400) — hover, subtle accents

### Neutral
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Gray 50: `#F8F9FA` — page backgrounds
- Gray 100: `#F1F3F5` — card backgrounds
- Gray 200: `#E9ECEF` — borders
- Gray 400: `#868E96` — muted text
- Gray 600: `#495057` — body text
- Gray 800: `#212529` — headings

### Status
- Success: `#2D7D46` — green for trust indicators
- Error: `#C0392B`

## Typography

### Font Family
- Headings: `Inter` (700, 800)
- Body: `Inter` (400, 500)

### Scale
- Hero H1: `text-4xl md:text-5xl lg:text-6xl font-bold`
- Section H2: `text-3xl md:text-4xl font-bold`
- Card H3: `text-xl font-semibold`
- Body: `text-base text-gray-600`
- Small/Caption: `text-sm text-gray-400`

## Spacing
- Section padding: `py-16 md:py-24`
- Container: `max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8`
- Card padding: `p-6 md:p-8`
- Gap between cards: `gap-6 md:gap-8`

## Borders & Shadows
- Card border: `border border-gray-200`
- Card radius: `rounded-xl`
- Card shadow: `shadow-sm hover:shadow-md transition-shadow`
- Button radius: `rounded-lg`

## Buttons
- Primary CTA: `bg-[#C8922A] hover:bg-[#E8B84B] text-white font-semibold px-6 py-3 rounded-lg`
- Secondary: `bg-[#1B3A6B] hover:bg-[#2A5298] text-white font-semibold px-6 py-3 rounded-lg`
- Outline: `border-2 border-[#1B3A6B] text-[#1B3A6B] hover:bg-[#1B3A6B] hover:text-white px-6 py-3 rounded-lg`

## Do's
- Use navy for trust, authority, and structure
- Use gold sparingly for CTAs and highlights
- Keep layouts clean with generous whitespace
- Use real-world imagery (factories, QC, shipping)
- Use numbered steps for process sections
- Use icons alongside text for scannability

## Don'ts
- No neon or overly bright colors
- No exaggerated claims in copy
- No cluttered layouts
- No low-contrast text on colored backgrounds
- No decorative fonts
