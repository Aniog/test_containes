# SSourcing China - Design System

## Brand Identity
- **Company**: SSourcing China - Professional B2B Sourcing Agent
- **Industry**: International Trade / Supply Chain Management
- **Target Audience**: Global buyers, importers, procurement managers

## Color Palette (Trust-focused B2B)
- **Primary Blue**: #1E40AF (Tailwind: blue-800) - Trust, reliability, professionalism
- **Dark Blue**: #1E3A5F - Headers, navigation
- **Accent Blue**: #3B82F6 (Tailwind: blue-500) - CTAs, highlights
- **Light Blue**: #EFF6FF (Tailwind: blue-50) - Backgrounds, cards
- **Dark Gray**: #1F2937 (Tailwind: gray-800) - Body text
- **Medium Gray**: #6B7280 (Tailwind: gray-500) - Secondary text
- **Light Gray**: #F9FAFB (Tailwind: gray-50) - Section backgrounds
- **White**: #FFFFFF - Primary background
- **Success Green**: #059669 (Tailwind: emerald-600) - Trust badges, positive indicators
- **Warning Amber**: #D97706 (Tailwind: amber-600) - Urgency elements

## Typography
- **Font Family**: Inter (Google Fonts) - Clean, professional, highly readable
- **Headings**: 
  - H1: text-4xl md:text-5xl lg:text-6xl font-bold
  - H2: text-3xl md:text-4xl font-bold
  - H3: text-xl md:text-2xl font-semibold
- **Body**: text-base md:text-lg leading-relaxed
- **Small/Caption**: text-sm

## Spacing
- **Section padding**: py-16 md:py-24
- **Container max-width**: max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8
- **Card padding**: p-6 md:p-8
- **Gap between elements**: gap-4 to gap-8

## Layout Patterns
- **Hero sections**: Full-width with subtle gradient or background image
- **Content sections**: Alternating white/gray-50 backgrounds
- **Cards**: White bg, subtle border (border-gray-200), rounded-xl, hover shadow
- **Grid layouts**: grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-3

## Visual Style
- **Borders**: border border-gray-200 (subtle)
- **Shadows**: shadow-sm for cards, shadow-lg for elevated elements
- **Border radius**: rounded-lg (8px) for cards, rounded-xl (12px) for larger elements
- **Hover effects**: transition-all duration-200, hover:shadow-md, hover:-translate-y-1

## Button Styles
- **Primary**: bg-blue-800 hover:bg-blue-900 text-white font-semibold rounded-lg px-6 py-3
- **Secondary**: bg-white border-2 border-blue-800 text-blue-800 hover:bg-blue-50 font-semibold rounded-lg px-6 py-3
- **Ghost**: text-blue-800 hover:bg-blue-50 font-medium

## Trust Elements
- Use checkmark icons (✓) for feature lists
- Include statistics with large numbers
- Partner/client logos in grayscale
- Certifications and badges prominently displayed
- Testimonials with company names and titles

## Do's
- Use plenty of whitespace
- Keep content scannable with clear headings
- Use icons to break up text
- Include CTAs in every section
- Show real statistics and numbers
- Use professional stock photos of factories, shipping, quality control

## Don'ts
- Don't use bright or playful colors
- Don't use casual or informal language
- Don't overuse animations or flashy effects
- Don't use stock photos of people shaking hands (overused in B2B)
- Don't clutter pages with too much text
- Don't use small, hard-to-read fonts
