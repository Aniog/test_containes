# MicroCosmos - Design Guide

## Visual Identity
A single-page website exploring the invisible microscopic universe. The design conveys wonder, scientific discovery, and the beauty of the tiny world around us.

## Typography
- Primary font: Inter (Google Fonts) - clean, modern, highly readable
- Headings: font-bold, tracking-tight
- Body: font-normal, leading-relaxed

## Color Palette
- Primary background: white (#ffffff) and near-white (#f8fafc)
- Dark sections: slate-900 (#0f172a) for contrast
- Primary accent: emerald-600 (#059669) - representing life and nature
- Secondary accent: cyan-500 (#06b6d4) - representing science and water
- Text primary: slate-800 (#1e293b)
- Text secondary: slate-500 (#64748b)
- Muted: slate-100 (#f1f5f9)

## Spacing
- Section padding: py-20 md:py-28
- Container max-w: max-w-6xl or max-w-7xl
- Card gaps: gap-6 md:gap-8
- Standard padding: px-4 md:px-8

## Visual Style
- Clean, modern, with generous whitespace
- Cards with subtle shadows and rounded corners (rounded-2xl)
- Smooth transitions on hover
- Use gradient overlays on hero images
- Alternating light and dark sections for visual rhythm
- Large, impactful images with proper aspect ratios

## Do's
- Use emerald and cyan as accent colors
- Keep text highly readable against backgrounds
- Use rounded-2xl for cards
- Maintain generous spacing between sections
- Use shadow-sm or shadow-md on cards

## Don'ts
- Don't use harsh neon colors
- Don't cram content too tightly
- Don't use text-shadow effects
- Don't use tiny font sizes
