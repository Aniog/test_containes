# SSourcing China Design Guidelines

## Visual Style
Clean, professional, and trustworthy B2B aesthetic. Focus on global trade, reliability, and precision.

## Typography
- **Heading Font**: Inter, sans-serif (Bold/Semi-bold)
- **Body Font**: Inter, sans-serif (Regular)
- **Base Size**: 16px

## Colors (Tailwind classes)
- **Primary**: Slate-900 (Professional, serious) `bg-slate-900`
- **Secondary**: Blue-600 (Trust, international) `bg-blue-600`
- **Accent**: Emerald-600 (Success, quality) `bg-emerald-600`
- **Background**: Slate-50 `bg-slate-50`
- **Text**: Slate-900 (Main), Slate-600 (Muted)

## Borders and Spacing
- **Border Radius**: Small to Medium (`rounded-md`, `rounded-lg`)
- **Container**: `max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8`
- **Section Spacing**: `py-16 md:py-24`

## Components Style
- **Buttons**: Sharp corners or slight rounding. Primary: Solid Blue-600. Secondary: Outline Slate-900.
- **Cards**: Subtle shadows (`shadow-sm`), clear borders (`border border-slate-200`).
- **Icons**: Lucide icons in Slate-600 or Blue-600.

## Do's and Don'ts
### Do's
- Use high-quality factory and logistics visuals.
- Maintain ample white space.
- Use clear, benefit-driven headlines.
- Show specific process steps.

### Don'ts
- Use cheesy stock photos of handshakes.
- Use overly bright or neon colors.
- Use cluttered layouts.
- Make unrealistic "cheapest price" claims.
