# Velmora Fine Jewelry Design System

## Direction
Velmora uses a warm editorial luxury aesthetic: soft ivory surfaces, deep espresso typography, muted sand neutrals, and restrained antique gold accents. The mood should feel quiet, premium, and intimate.

## Color palette
Use these named colors consistently through Tailwind tokens:
- `velmora-ivory` `#f7f1ea` — primary site background
- `velmora-card` `#fcf8f3` — cards, panels, elevated surfaces
- `velmora-sand` `#eadfce` — soft dividers, muted blocks, secondary backgrounds
- `velmora-blush` `#dcc4b3` — warm accent background for newsletter and subtle highlights
- `velmora-ink` `#1c1714` — primary text and dark surfaces
- `velmora-cocoa` `#2d241e` — hero overlay, footer, deeper backgrounds
- `velmora-smoke` `#6f6257` — secondary text
- `velmora-gold` `#b08a47` — accent buttons, stars, premium emphasis
- `velmora-bronze` `#8c6731` — hover and pressed accent states

## Typography
- Headings and product names: `Cormorant Garamond`
- Body, navigation, UI, forms: `Inter`
- Product names should be uppercase with wide tracking.

## Layout and spacing
- Use generous whitespace.
- Keep content centered with a wide but elegant max-width.
- Use thin borders and hairline dividers instead of heavy frames.
- Prefer airy desktop layouts and compact but breathable mobile spacing.

## Components
- Buttons should feel premium: solid gold on dark text or refined outlined styles.
- Cards should have soft shadows and subtle hover elevation.
- Editorial imagery should be large, warm, and calm.
- Use restrained transitions around 200–300ms.

## Do
- Use explicit readable foreground colors on every surface.
- Keep sections calm, balanced, and premium.
- Let imagery and typography breathe.

## Don’t
- Do not use loud promotional colors.
- Do not use crowded layouts or discount-style badges.
- Do not rely on low-contrast muted text for important information.
