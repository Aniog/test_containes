# SSourcing China - B2B Website Design System

## Visual Style
- Clean, professional, trustworthy B2B aesthetic
- International business tone - no flashy or exaggerated elements
- Factory/QC/shipping imagery to convey reliability and expertise

## Colors
- Primary: `#0F4C81` (classic blue - trust, professionalism)
- Primary dark: `#0A3659`
- Primary light: `#E8F1F8`
- Accent: `#E8792B` (warm orange - action, energy for CTAs)
- Accent hover: `#D4691F`
- Text primary: `#1A2B3C` (dark navy)
- Text secondary: `#5A6B7C` (muted gray-blue)
- Text light: `#8A9BAC`
- Background: `#FFFFFF` (white)
- Background alt: `#F7F9FC` (light gray-blue)
- Border: `#E2E8F0`
- Success: `#10B981`
- Warning: `#F59E0B`

## Typography
- Font family: Inter (Google Fonts)
- Headings: 600-700 weight
- Body: 400-500 weight
- Hero H1: 48-56px desktop, 32-40px mobile
- Section H2: 32-36px desktop, 24-28px mobile
- H3: 20-24px
- Body: 16px
- Small: 14px

## Spacing
- Section padding: 80px vertical desktop, 48px mobile
- Container max-width: 1200px
- Grid gap: 24-32px
- Card padding: 24-32px

## Components
- Buttons: rounded-lg (8px), proper padding
- Cards: rounded-xl (12px), subtle shadow, white background
- Inputs: rounded-lg, border-gray-200, focus ring primary
- Badges: rounded-full, small text

## Do's
- Use plenty of white space
- Keep text concise and scannable
- Use icons to support text content
- Show real factory/QC/shipping imagery
- Maintain consistent spacing rhythm

## Don'ts
- No exaggerated claims or hype language
- No cluttered layouts
- No bright/neon colors
- No overly decorative elements
- No dark mode (B2B prefers light, clean)
