# Velmora Fine Jewelry Visual System

## Direction
Quiet luxury with a warm editorial mood. The store should feel calm, premium, feminine, and curated rather than promotional. Imagery is large and tactile, with gold jewelry against warm skin, ivory fabric, espresso shadows, and soft champagne light.

## Palette
Use one consistent soft-neutral direction with deep espresso contrast and restrained antique-gold accents.

- Porcelain base: `velmora-porcelain` (`#F7F1E8`) for page backgrounds.
- Champagne surface: `velmora-champagne` (`#EFE2CF`) for panels and soft blocks.
- Sand surface: `velmora-sand` (`#D8C4A8`) for subtle secondary surfaces.
- Espresso text/base: `velmora-espresso` (`#211713`) for primary text and dark sections.
- Cocoa: `velmora-cocoa` (`#4B3329`) for secondary dark copy and borders on light surfaces.
- Antique gold: `velmora-gold` (`#B68A48`) for CTAs, icons, star ratings, and restrained accents.
- Soft gold: `velmora-softgold` (`#D7B978`) for hover states and hairline details.
- Ivory: `velmora-ivory` (`#FFFDF8`) for cards and readable content surfaces.

## Typography
- Headings and logo: Cormorant Garamond, elegant serif, generous line height.
- Body and UI: Manrope, clean sans-serif.
- Product names: uppercase, wide letter spacing, serif, refined scale.

## Layout and spacing
- Mobile-first with generous vertical rhythm.
- Use max-width containers with roomy horizontal padding.
- Use thin hairline dividers in cocoa/gold at low opacity.
- Cards should feel editorial: soft shadows, rounded corners, calm hover motion.

## Components
- Primary buttons: antique-gold background with espresso text, uppercase small label, wide tracking.
- Secondary buttons: transparent/ivory with thin cocoa or gold border.
- Product cards: ivory background, image-led, minimal metadata, hover reveals quick add.
- Cart drawer: ivory surface, readable espresso text, gold CTA.

## Do
- Keep whitespace generous.
- Keep contrast strong and text explicitly readable.
- Use warm, jewelry-specific stock image prompts via Strikingly image tags.
- Use smooth transitions, never flashy effects.

## Don't
- Do not use discount colors, bright reds, neon gradients, or generic marketplace styling.
- Do not crowd grids or rely on tiny low-contrast text.
- Do not hardcode image URLs.
