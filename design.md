# SSourcing China - Design Guidelines

## Brand Overview
SSourcing China is a professional China-based sourcing agent helping overseas B2B buyers find reliable suppliers, verify factories, inspect quality, follow production, and coordinate shipping.

Tone: Professional, clear, practical, trustworthy. No exaggerated claims.

## Visual Style
- Clean, modern B2B aesthetic
- Trustworthy color palette: deep navy, slate gray, white, subtle teal accents
- Generous whitespace, clear hierarchy
- Professional typography with Inter font family
- Realistic factory, QC, and logistics imagery

## Color Palette
- Primary: #0F172A (slate-900) - Deep navy for headers
- Secondary: #1E293B (slate-800) - Dark slate for text
- Accent: #0D9488 (teal-600) - Professional teal for CTAs and highlights
- Accent Light: #14B8A6 (teal-500)
- Background: #FFFFFF (white) and #F8FAFC (slate-50)
- Muted: #64748B (slate-500) - Secondary text
- Border: #E2E8F0 (slate-200)
- Success/Trust: #059669 (emerald-600)

## Typography
- Font: Inter (system-ui fallback)
- Headings: 600-700 weight, tight tracking
- Body: 400-500 weight, 1.6 line-height
- Small text: 14px, slate-500

## Spacing
- Section padding: py-16 md:py-20
- Container max-width: 1280px (mx-auto px-6)
- Card gaps: gap-6 or gap-8
- Consistent 4px grid

## Components
- Buttons: Solid primary (teal), outline secondary, large for CTAs
- Cards: White bg, subtle border, rounded-xl, shadow-sm
- Forms: Clean inputs with focus states, proper labels
- Navigation: Clean top bar, active states, mobile hamburger

## Imagery
- Use data-strk-img and data-strk-bg for all visuals
- Realistic: factories, inspection, containers, warehouses, meetings
- Professional, not stock-photo generic

## Page Structure
All pages share:
- Top navigation (logo + links + CTA)
- Footer with links and contact
- Consistent container widths

## Responsive
- Mobile-first approach
- Desktop: multi-column layouts
- Tablet: adjusted grids
- Mobile: stacked, hamburger menu

## Forms
- Inquiry form on Home and Contact
- Fields: name, company, email, phone, product/category, message, quantity estimate
- Validation: required fields, email format
- Success: toast confirmation (frontend only)

## SEO
- Title: China Sourcing Agent | Supplier Verification, QC & Shipping | SSourcing China
- Meta description: Professional China sourcing agent providing supplier verification, quality control, and shipping coordination for global B2B buyers.
