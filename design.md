# Design System: SSourcing China

## Visual Style
The brand voice is professional, trustworthy, and international. The design should reflect clinical efficiency, transparency, and deep local expertise in China.

## Colors
- **Primary:** Deep Navy Blue (#0A2540) - Symbolizes trust and stability.
- **Secondary:** Industrial Gold/Amber (#F59E0B) - Represents China/Manufacturing and adds a call-to-action accent.
- **Neutral:** Clean White (#FFFFFF), Soft Gray (#F8FAFC), Dark Slate (#1E293B).
- **Success:** Forest Green (#10B981) - For "Reliable" and "Verified" indicators.

## Typography
- **Headings:** Inter (Sans-serif) - Bold, professional, clear.
- **Body:** Inter (Sans-serif) - Readable, clean, modern.

## Components
- **Buttons:**
  - Primary: `bg-amber-500 hover:bg-amber-600 text-white font-semibold py-3 px-6 rounded-md transition-all shadow-md`
  - Secondary: `border-2 border-slate-800 text-slate-800 hover:bg-slate-800 hover:text-white font-semibold py-3 px-6 rounded-md transition-all`
- **Cards:** White background, subtle shadow (`shadow-sm`), rounded corners (`rounded-lg`), clean borders (`border border-slate-100`).
- **Icons:** Lucide React icons in primary or success colors.

## Spacing
- Use Tailwind's spacing scale (e.g., `p-4`, `m-8`, `gap-6`).
- Section padding: `py-20` for desktop, `py-12` for mobile.

## Mobile Design
- Stacked layouts for forms and grids.
- Sticky navigation with mobile menu.
- Large, touch-friendly CTA buttons.
