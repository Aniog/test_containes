# SSourcing China Design System

## Brand direction
A clean, trustworthy, international B2B visual style for overseas buyers looking for sourcing support in China. The design should feel practical, efficient, and credible rather than flashy.

## Typography
- Primary font: Inter
- Headings: semi-bold to bold, tight tracking, neutral dark text
- Body text: regular weight, comfortable line height
- Example Tailwind classes: `font-sans`, `text-slate-950`, `text-slate-700`, `tracking-tight`

## Color system
- Primary ink: deep slate for credibility
- Secondary text: muted slate
- Accent: professional blue for links and CTAs
- Surface: white and very light slate backgrounds
- Success: restrained green
- Example Tailwind classes: `bg-slate-950`, `text-slate-950`, `text-slate-700`, `bg-slate-50`, `border-slate-200`, `bg-blue-600`, `hover:bg-blue-700`, `text-white`

## Layout and spacing
- Use generous whitespace and clear section separation
- Keep content in a centered container with strong desktop structure
- Use 2- to 4-column layouts on desktop and stack on mobile
- Example Tailwind classes: `mx-auto max-w-7xl px-6 lg:px-8`, `py-16 lg:py-24`, `gap-6 lg:gap-10`

## Surfaces
- Cards should use white backgrounds, subtle borders, rounded corners, and light shadows
- Avoid heavy gradients or glassmorphism
- Example Tailwind classes: `rounded-3xl border border-slate-200 bg-white shadow-sm`

## Buttons
- Primary buttons: blue background with white text
- Secondary buttons: white background with slate border and dark text
- Example Tailwind classes: `inline-flex items-center justify-center rounded-full bg-blue-600 px-6 py-3 text-sm font-semibold text-white hover:bg-blue-700`, `rounded-full border border-slate-300 bg-white px-6 py-3 text-sm font-semibold text-slate-900`

## Imagery
- Use realistic sourcing-related visuals: factories, supplier meetings, quality inspection, cargo and shipping
- Images should support trust and capability, not feel generic lifestyle-oriented

## Do
- Keep all text clearly readable on every surface
- Use concise, business-focused copy blocks
- Emphasize process clarity, practical support, and trust signals

## Don't
- Do not use exaggerated claims or hype language
- Do not use overly dark pages or low-contrast text
- Do not use decorative visuals that distract from inquiry generation
