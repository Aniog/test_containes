# Visual Style Guide - Velmora Fine Jewelry

## Mood
Quiet luxury, warm, editorial, premium.

## Typography
- Headings & Product Names: Serif (Cormorant Garamond)
  - Product names: UPPERCASE, wide letter-spacing (`tracking-[0.2em]`)
- Body & UI: Sans-serif (Inter)

## Color Palette
- Base: Deep Refined Neutral (`bg-[#1a1a1a]`) or Soft Editorial Neutral (`bg-[#f9f7f2]`)
- Accents: Warm Gold/Metallic (`text-[#d4af37]`), charcoal, ivory.
- Dividers: Thin hairline (`border-[#e5e5e5]` or `border-stone-200/50`)

## Standard Visuals
- Generous whitespace (`p-8`, `gap-12`)
- Large editorial imagery
- Soft hover transitions (`transition-all duration-500 ease-in-out`)
- Restrained accent colors
- Buttons: Premium feel, solid or outlined.

## Layout
- Sticky transparent navigation
- Full-bleed hero sections
- Responsive grids (1 col mobile, 3-4 col desktop)
