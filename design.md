# SSourcing China Visual Style Guide

SSourcing China should feel clean, trustworthy, practical, and international. The site is aimed at overseas B2B buyers who need reliable China sourcing support, so the design should prioritize clarity, confidence, and easy inquiry generation.

## Brand personality
- Professional, calm, and operationally competent.
- Practical and specific rather than flashy or exaggerated.
- International B2B tone with subtle China trade context.

## Color direction
- Base surfaces: white, slate-50, slate-100.
- Primary business color: slate-950 / slate-900 for headings and navigation.
- Trust accent: blue-700 and blue-600 for CTAs, links, and important highlights.
- Supporting accent: amber-500 / amber-100 for quality-check and logistics highlights.
- Success/trust cues: emerald-700 / emerald-50 for verification and process details.
- Avoid loud gradients, neon colors, and low-contrast text.

## Typography
- Use Inter from Google Fonts for a clean international business look.
- Headings should be bold, tight, and readable: examples `text-4xl md:text-6xl font-bold tracking-tight`.
- Body copy should be clear and restrained: examples `text-base md:text-lg leading-7 text-slate-600`.
- Labels and small metadata should use uppercase tracking sparingly.

## Layout and spacing
- Use generous section spacing: `py-16 md:py-24`.
- Use centered max-width containers: `max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8`.
- Desktop layouts should use multi-column grids; mobile should stack cleanly.
- Cards should use `rounded-2xl`, `border border-slate-200`, white backgrounds, and subtle shadows.

## Visuals
- Use realistic factory, inspection, product sourcing, warehouse, and shipping imagery.
- Images should support trust and operational competence, not feel like generic lifestyle marketing.
- Use the Strikingly stock image tagging system with nearby text references.

## Components
- CTAs: primary button `bg-blue-700 text-white hover:bg-blue-800`; secondary button `border border-slate-300 text-slate-900 hover:bg-slate-50`.
- Forms: white cards, clear labels, visible borders, and helpful placeholder text.
- Badges: muted slate/blue/emerald surfaces with explicit readable text.

## Do's
- Keep copy direct and specific.
- Make inquiry actions easy to find.
- Use visible text colors on every custom background.
- Include practical trust points: supplier checks, QC reporting, production follow-up, shipment coordination.

## Don'ts
- Do not use exaggerated claims such as guaranteed best price or risk-free sourcing.
- Do not use decorative visuals that distract from lead generation.
- Do not leave form text, card text, or overlay text with implicit or low-contrast colors.
