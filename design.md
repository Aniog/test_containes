# SSourcing China - Design System

## Brand Identity
- Professional B2B sourcing company based in China
- Trustworthy, international, clean aesthetic
- Target audience: overseas buyers looking for China sourcing support

## Colors
- Primary: `brand-navy` (#1B2B4B) - trust, professionalism
- Secondary: `brand-blue` (#2563EB) - action, links, CTAs
- Accent: `brand-orange` (#F97316) - highlights, urgency
- Background: `brand-light` (#F8FAFC) - clean white-ish
- Surface: `brand-white` (#FFFFFF) - cards, sections
- Text primary: `brand-dark` (#1E293B) - headings
- Text secondary: `brand-gray` (#64748B) - body text
- Text muted: `brand-muted` (#94A3B8) - captions
- Border: `brand-border` (#E2E8F0)
- Success: `brand-green` (#10B981)

## Typography
- Font: Inter (Google Fonts)
- Headings: font-bold, tracking-tight
  - H1: text-4xl md:text-5xl lg:text-6xl
  - H2: text-3xl md:text-4xl
  - H3: text-xl md:text-2xl
  - H4: text-lg md:text-xl
- Body: text-base, text-brand-gray, leading-relaxed
- Small: text-sm, text-brand-muted

## Spacing
- Section padding: py-16 md:py-24
- Container: max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8
- Card padding: p-6 md:p-8
- Gap between cards: gap-6 md:gap-8

## Components
- Buttons primary: bg-brand-blue text-white px-6 py-3 rounded-lg font-semibold hover:bg-blue-700 transition
- Buttons secondary: border-2 border-brand-blue text-brand-blue px-6 py-3 rounded-lg font-semibold hover:bg-brand-blue hover:text-white transition
- Cards: bg-brand-white rounded-xl shadow-sm border border-brand-border p-6 md:p-8
- Badges: bg-blue-50 text-brand-blue text-sm font-medium px-3 py-1 rounded-full

## Layout
- Desktop: multi-column grids (2-3-4 cols)
- Mobile: single column stacking
- Max content width: max-w-7xl
- Navigation: sticky top, white bg, shadow-sm

## Do's
- Use plenty of whitespace
- Keep text concise and scannable
- Use icons from Lucide for visual support
- Use stock images for factory, QC, shipping contexts
- Maintain consistent card heights in grids

## Don'ts
- No dark mode
- No overly decorative elements
- No exaggerated marketing claims
- No magic hex values outside the defined palette
- No cramped layouts on mobile
