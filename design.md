# Visual Style Guide - SSourcing China

## Typography
- **Primary Font**: Inter (system-ui, sans-serif)
- **Headings**: Semi-bold to Bold (600-700), clean and professional.
- **Body**: Regular (400), readable line height (1.6).

## Colors
- **Primary**: Deep Blue (`#0f172a`, `slate-900`) - Represents trust and stability.
- **Secondary**: Professional Blue (`#3b82f6`, `blue-500`) - Accents and CTA.
- **Accent**: Gold/Amber (`#f59e0b`, `amber-500`) - For highlighting quality or certification.
- **Background**: 
  - Main: White (`#ffffff`)
  - Sections: Off-white (`#f8fafc`, `slate-50`) or Light Gray (`#f1f5f9`, `slate-100`)
- **Text**:
  - Headings: Dark Blue/Slate (`#0f172a`, `slate-900`)
  - Body: Muted Slate (`#475569`, `slate-600`)

## Spacing & Layout
- **Containers**: Standard max-width container (`max-w-7xl`) for desktop.
- **Padding**: Generous vertical padding for sections (`py-16` to `py-24`).
- **Grid**: Responsive 1, 2, or 3 column grids for features and services.

## Components Style (shadcn/ui)
- **Buttons**: Rounded-md, solid primary colors for main CTAs.
- **Cards**: Subtle shadows (`shadow-sm`), white background, thin borders (`border-slate-200`).
- **Inputs**: Clean, outlined, with clear labels and placeholders.

## Imagery
- **Focus**: Realistic factory floors, quality inspection equipment, shipping containers, professional sourcing team interactions.
- **Style**: High resolution, clean composition, natural lighting.

## Do's and Don'ts
### Do's
- Keep the design clean and uncluttered.
- Use professional icons (Lucide).
- Ensure high contrast for readability.
- Maintain a consistent professional tone.

### Don'ts
- Use overly bright or neon colors.
- Use generic "cartoonish" illustrations; prefer professional photography styles.
- Overcrowd sections with too much text.
