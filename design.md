# SSourcing China – Design System

## Brand Identity
- Company: SSourcing China
- Tagline: China Sourcing Agent for Global Buyers
- Tone: Professional, trustworthy, international, practical

## Color Palette
- Primary (Deep Navy): `#1B2B4B` – `bg-navy-900`
- Accent (China Red): `#C0392B` – `bg-red-700`
- Accent Light (Gold): `#D4A017` – `bg-gold`
- Background Light: `#F5F7FA` – `bg-slate-50`
- Background White: `#FFFFFF`
- Text Primary: `#1B2B4B` – `text-navy-900`
- Text Secondary: `#4A5568` – `text-slate-600`
- Text Muted: `#718096` – `text-slate-500`
- Border: `#E2E8F0` – `border-slate-200`
- Success: `#2D7D46` – `text-green-700`

## Typography
- Font Family: Inter (Google Fonts)
- Headings: font-bold, tracking-tight
- H1: text-4xl md:text-5xl lg:text-6xl
- H2: text-3xl md:text-4xl
- H3: text-xl md:text-2xl
- Body: text-base (16px), leading-relaxed
- Small/Caption: text-sm

## Spacing
- Section padding: py-16 md:py-24
- Container: max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8
- Card padding: p-6 md:p-8
- Gap between cards: gap-6 md:gap-8

## Components
- Primary Button: bg-red-700 hover:bg-red-800 text-white font-semibold px-6 py-3 rounded-lg
- Secondary Button: border-2 border-navy-900 text-navy-900 hover:bg-navy-900 hover:text-white px-6 py-3 rounded-lg
- Card: bg-white rounded-xl shadow-sm border border-slate-200 p-6
- Badge: bg-red-50 text-red-700 text-xs font-semibold px-3 py-1 rounded-full
- Section Label: text-red-700 font-semibold text-sm uppercase tracking-widest

## Do's
- Use navy for headers and footers
- Use red accent sparingly for CTAs and highlights
- Use generous whitespace
- Use real-looking B2B imagery (factories, QC, shipping)
- Keep copy concise and benefit-focused

## Don'ts
- No neon colors
- No rounded-full on large buttons
- No exaggerated claims ("best in the world")
- No cluttered layouts
