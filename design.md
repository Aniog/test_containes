# SSourcing China - Design Guidelines

## Brand Identity
- **Company**: SSourcing China
- **Industry**: B2B Sourcing / Supply Chain
- **Tone**: Professional, trustworthy, clear, practical — no exaggerated claims

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors
- **Navy Blue** (primary): `#1e3a5f` — Trust, professionalism, B2B authority
- **Deep Blue** (primary-dark): `#0f2440` — Headers, dark backgrounds
- **Sky Blue** (primary-light): `#3b82f6` — Links, accents, interactive elements
- **Bright Blue** (accent): `#2563eb` — CTA buttons, highlights

### Neutral Colors
- **White**: `#ffffff` — Backgrounds, cards
- **Light Gray**: `#f8fafc` — Section backgrounds (alternating)
- **Medium Gray**: `#e2e8f0` — Borders, dividers
- **Slate**: `#64748b` — Secondary text
- **Dark Slate**: `#334155` — Body text
- **Near Black**: `#0f172a` — Headings

### Accent Colors
- **Green** (success/trust): `#16a34a` — Trust badges, checkmarks
- **Amber** (warning/attention): `#f59e0b` — Subtle highlights
- **Red** (urgency): `#dc2626` — Limited use for important notices

## Typography
- **Font Family**: Inter (Google Fonts)
- **Headings**: font-weight 700-800, tracking tight
- **Body**: font-weight 400, line-height 1.6-1.75
- **H1**: 3rem (48px) on desktop, 2rem (32px) on mobile
- **H2**: 2.25rem (36px) on desktop, 1.75rem (28px) on mobile
- **H3**: 1.5rem (24px) on desktop, 1.25rem (20px) on mobile
- **Body**: 1rem (16px)
- **Small**: 0.875rem (14px)

## Spacing
- Section padding: `py-20` (80px) on desktop, `py-12` (48px) on mobile
- Container max-width: `max-w-7xl` (1280px)
- Card padding: `p-6` to `p-8`
- Element gaps: `gap-6` to `gap-8`

## Visual Style
- Clean, minimal design with generous whitespace
- Card-based layouts with subtle shadows (`shadow-sm` to `shadow-md`)
- Rounded corners: `rounded-lg` (8px) for cards, `rounded-md` (6px) for buttons
- Alternating white and light gray section backgrounds
- Professional stock imagery: factories, quality control, shipping containers, logistics
- Icons from Lucide React for service features and process steps

## Component Patterns
- **Buttons**: Primary (blue bg, white text), Secondary (outline blue), Ghost (text only)
- **Cards**: White bg, subtle border, hover shadow transition
- **Navigation**: Sticky top, white bg with bottom border, navy links
- **Footer**: Dark navy bg, white/light text
- **Forms**: Clean inputs with labels, blue focus rings
- **FAQ**: Accordion-style expandable sections

## Do's
- Use navy blue as the dominant brand color
- Keep layouts clean with ample whitespace
- Use professional, industry-relevant imagery
- Ensure high contrast for readability
- Use consistent spacing and alignment
- Make CTAs prominent and clear

## Don'ts
- Don't use bright or playful colors
- Don't use cartoon illustrations
- Don't use exaggerated marketing language
- Don't crowd sections with too much content
- Don't use low-contrast text
- Don't use more than 2-3 colors per section
