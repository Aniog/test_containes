# Design System - SSourcing China

## Visual Style
- **Professional, clean, trustworthy, and international B2B.**
- **Color Palette:**
  - Primary: Deep Blue (Trust, Reliability) - `#1e3a8a` (Tailwind `blue-900`)
  - Secondary: Gold/Amber (Quality, Value) - `#d97706` (Tailwind `amber-600`)
  - Accent: Slate/Gray (Industrial, Practical) - `#475569` (Tailwind `slate-600`)
  - Background: Off-white/Light Gray - `#f8fafc` (Tailwind `slate-50`)
  - Text: Dark Slate - `#1e293b` (Tailwind `slate-800`)
- **Typography:**
  - Headings: Inter, Sans-serif (Bold, clear)
  - Body: Inter, Sans-serif (Readable, professional)
- **Spacing & Layout:**
  - Generous whitespace for readability.
  - Consistent padding (Tailwind `p-4`, `p-8`, `p-12`).
  - Max-width containers for desktop (Tailwind `max-w-7xl`).
- **Components:**
  - Shadowed cards for trust points and case studies.
  - Bordered inputs for forms.
  - Clear CTA buttons with high contrast.

## Do's and Don'ts
### Do's
- Use high-quality, realistic sourcing/factory visuals via `data-strk-img`.
- Keep the language clear, practical, and direct.
- Highlight "Made in China" expertise responsibly.
- Use iconography (Lucide React) to represent services.

### Don'ts
- Use exaggerated claims or "get rich quick" tone.
- Use overly bright or neon colors.
- Crowded layouts on mobile.
- Low-contrast text.
