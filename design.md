# Design Guidelines - SSourcing China

## Visual Style
*   **Theme**: Professional, Trustworthy, International B2B.
*   **Colors**:
    *   Primary: Deep Navy Blue (`#1a2b3c`) - representing stability and professionalism.
    *   Secondary: Professional Blue (`#2c5282`) - for accents and highlights.
    *   Accent: Gold/Industrial Yellow (`#d4af37`) - representing quality and value (use sparingly for CTAs).
    *   Background: Clean White (`#ffffff`) and Light Gray (`#f7fafc`) for sections.
    *   Text: Charcoal Gray (`#2d3748`) for readability.
*   **Typography**:
    *   Heading: Inter, sans-serif (Bold, semi-bold).
    *   Body: Inter, sans-serif (Regular, 400).
*   **Spacing**: Generous whitespace to maintain a clean, high-end feel.
*   **Borders**: Subtle borders (`border-gray-200`) and rounded corners (`rounded-lg`).
*   **Shadows**: Soft shadows (`shadow-sm`, `shadow-md`) for cards.

## Component Styles
*   **Buttons**:
    *   Primary: `bg-navy-900 text-white hover:bg-navy-800 transition-colors px-6 py-3 rounded-md font-semibold` (Custom navy color to be added).
    *   Secondary: `border-2 border-navy-900 text-navy-900 hover:bg-navy-50 px-6 py-3 rounded-md font-semibold`.
*   **Cards**: `bg-white border border-gray-100 rounded-xl p-6 shadow-sm hover:shadow-md transition-shadow`.
*   **Input Fields**: `w-full px-4 py-2 border border-gray-300 rounded-md focus:ring-2 focus:ring-blue-500 focus:border-transparent outline-none`.
