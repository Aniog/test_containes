# SSourcing China - Design System

## Visual Style
- Clean, professional, international B2B aesthetic
- Trustworthy, no exaggerated claims
- High contrast for readability
- Generous white space

## Colors
- Primary: brand-500 (#1e3a5f) - Dark navy blue
- Primary light: brand-50 (#eff6ff) - Light blue backgrounds
- Accent: accent-500 (#d97706) - Amber/gold for CTAs and highlights
- Text: gray-900 for headings, gray-600 for body
- Backgrounds: white for content, gray-50 for alternate sections

## Typography
- Font: Inter (sans-serif)
- Headings: font-bold, tracking-tight
- Body: font-normal, leading-relaxed
- Section headings: text-3xl md:text-4xl font-bold
- Subheadings: text-lg md:text-xl text-gray-600

## Spacing
- Section padding: py-16 md:py-24
- Container max-width: max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8
- Card padding: p-6 md:p-8
- Gap between cards: gap-6 md:gap-8

## Borders & Shadows
- Cards: rounded-xl, shadow-sm border border-gray-100
- Hover: shadow-md transition-shadow
- Buttons: rounded-lg

## Components
- CTA buttons: bg-accent-500 hover:bg-accent-600 text-white font-semibold px-6 py-3 rounded-lg
- Secondary buttons: border-2 border-brand-500 text-brand-500 hover:bg-brand-50
- Section backgrounds: alternate between white and gray-50
- Feature icons: rounded-xl bg-brand-50 p-3 w-12 h-12

## Do's
- Use ample white space
- Keep consistent spacing
- Use professional, muted tones
- High contrast text on all backgrounds
- Clean, minimal layouts

## Don'ts
- No bright, flashy colors
- No exaggerated claims
- No cluttered layouts
- No low contrast text
