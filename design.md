# SSourcing China - Design Guidelines

## Overview
Professional B2B website for a China-based sourcing agent helping overseas buyers find reliable suppliers, verify factories, inspect quality, follow production, and coordinate shipping.

## Typography
- **Primary Font**: Inter (Google Fonts)
- **Headings**: 
  - H1: 48px/56px, font-weight 700
  - H2: 36px/44px, font-weight 600
  - H3: 24px/32px, font-weight 600
  - H4: 20px/28px, font-weight 500
- **Body**: 16px/24px, font-weight 400
- **Small**: 14px/20px, font-weight 400

## Color Palette
- **Primary**: #1E3A5F (Deep Navy Blue - trust, professionalism)
- **Primary Light**: #2D5A8A
- **Secondary**: #F97316 (Orange - CTAs, energy)
- **Secondary Hover**: #EA580C
- **Accent**: #10B981 (Green - success, reliability)
- **Background**: #FFFFFF
- **Background Alt**: #F8FAFC
- **Background Dark**: #0F172A
- **Text Primary**: #1E293B
- **Text Secondary**: #64748B
- **Text Light**: #94A3B8
- **Border**: #E2E8F0

## Spacing System
- **Section Padding**: 80px vertical (desktop), 48px (mobile)
- **Container Max Width**: 1200px
- **Grid Gap**: 24px (desktop), 16px (mobile)
- **Component Padding**: 24px

## Visual Style
- Clean, professional B2B aesthetic
- Card-based layouts with subtle shadows
- Rounded corners: 8px (buttons), 12px (cards)
- Subtle hover animations
- Professional imagery: factory floors, QC inspections, shipping containers, warehouse operations

## Do's
- Use clear, professional imagery
- Maintain consistent spacing
- Use blue/navy for trust, orange for CTAs
- Include social proof (case studies, stats)
- Make forms prominent for lead generation

## Don'ts
- Avoid overly flashy animations
- Don't use too many colors
- Avoid cluttered layouts
- Don't exaggerate claims
- Keep text professional and practical
