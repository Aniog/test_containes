# SSourcing China - Design System

## Visual Style
Clean, professional, trustworthy B2B aesthetic. Dark navy + gold accent color scheme. 
International, modern, and confident without being flashy.

## Colors
- Primary background: White (#ffffff) for main content areas
- Dark sections: Navy blue (primary-900, primary-800)
- Text: primary-900 for headings, neutral-600/700 for body text
- Accent: Gold (accent-500: #ca8a04) for CTAs, highlights, and key accents
- Light backgrounds: primary-50 (#eff6ff) for alternate sections
- Footer: neutral-900 (#0f0f1a)

## Typography
- Font: Inter (Google Fonts) - loaded from index.html
- H1: text-4xl md:text-5xl lg:text-6xl, font-bold, tracking-tight
- H2: text-3xl md:text-4xl, font-bold, tracking-tight
- H3: text-xl md:text-2xl, font-semibold
- Body: text-base md:text-lg, leading-relaxed
- Small/caption: text-sm, text-neutral-500

## Spacing
- Section padding: py-16 md:py-24
- Container max-width: max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8
- Grid gaps: gap-8 md:gap-12
- Card padding: p-6 md:p-8

## Borders & Shadows
- Cards: bg-white rounded-xl shadow-sm border border-neutral-100
- Hover: shadow-md transition-shadow duration-300
- Inputs: rounded-lg border border-neutral-300 focus:ring-2 focus:ring-primary-500 focus:border-primary-500

## Buttons
- Primary CTA: bg-accent-500 hover:bg-accent-600 text-white font-semibold rounded-lg px-6 py-3 md:px-8 md:py-4 transition-all duration-200 shadow-md hover:shadow-lg
- Secondary: bg-primary-900 hover:bg-primary-800 text-white font-semibold rounded-lg px-6 py-3 transition-all duration-200
- Outline: border-2 border-primary-900 text-primary-900 hover:bg-primary-900 hover:text-white font-semibold rounded-lg px-6 py-3 transition-all duration-200

## Navigation
- Fixed top, white background with shadow-sm on scroll
- Logo: bold, brand name
- Links: primary-700 hover:text-accent-500 transition
- Mobile: hamburger menu with slide-out drawer

## Images
- Use data-strk-img attributes for stock images
- Hero image: 16x9 ratio, 1600px width - factory/warehouse/China business theme
- Section images: 16x9 or 4x3 ratio, 800px width
- Icons: Lucide React icons

## Do's
- Use ample white space
- Keep text left-aligned for readability
- Use gold accent sparingly for CTAs and highlights
- Show trust signals (badges, certifications, numbers)
- Use clean, professional imagery
- Maintain consistent spacing

## Don'ts
- Don't use overly bright colors
- Don't use stock photos of people smiling at cameras (use realistic factory/industrial shots)
- Don't make exaggerated claims or superlatives
- Don't overcrowd sections
- Don't use Comic Sans or playful fonts
- Don't use gradients
