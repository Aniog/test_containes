# Velmora Fine Jewelry — Design System

## Brand Identity
Quiet luxury, warm editorial, premium demi-fine jewelry. NOT loud, NOT discount, NOT generic.

## Color Palette
- `velmora-black` #1a1714 — primary text, dark backgrounds
- `velmora-charcoal` #2c2824 — secondary dark surfaces
- `velmora-espresso` #3d3530 — tertiary dark, borders
- `velmora-warm-gray` #8a7e76 — muted text, icons
- `velmora-sand` #c8bfb4 — dividers, subtle accents
- `velmora-cream` #f5f0eb — light backgrounds, cards
- `velmora-ivory` #faf8f5 — page background
- `velmora-gold` #b8963e — primary accent, CTAs
- `velmora-gold-light` #d4b062 — hover states
- `velmora-gold-dark` #96742e — pressed states
- `velmora-rose` #e8d5c4 — soft accent, highlights

## Typography
- Headings: Cormorant Garamond (serif), weights 300–700
- Body/UI: Inter (sans-serif), weights 300–600
- Product names: UPPERCASE with tracking-[0.15em]
- Prices: Inter, semibold

## Spacing
- Generous whitespace, 64–120px section padding on desktop
- 32–48px on mobile
- Thin hairline dividers (border-velmora-sand/30)

## Components
- Buttons: solid gold or outlined with gold border, rounded-pill
- Cards: velmora-cream bg, subtle shadow-card, rounded-lg
- Inputs: clean borders, rounded-md, focus:ring-gold
- Badges: small, rounded-pill, gold or charcoal bg

## Animations
- Hover: scale(1.02), shadow increase, 300ms ease-luxury
- Page load: fade-in with translateY(12px)
- Drawer: slide-in-right
- Transitions: 200–400ms, cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.1, 0.25, 1)

## Don'ts
- Don't use bright/primary colors (red, blue, green)
- Don't use hard black (#000) — use velmora-black instead
- Don't use border-radius larger than 12px on cards
- Don't use overly bold font weights in body text
