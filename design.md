# SSourcing China - Design System

## Brand Identity
- **Company**: SSourcing China
- **Industry**: B2B Sourcing Agent
- **Target Audience**: International buyers looking for Chinese suppliers
- **Tone**: Professional, trustworthy, practical, international

## Colors

### Primary Palette
- **Primary Blue**: `#0B3D6B` - Deep navy blue for trust and professionalism
- **Primary Dark**: `#062847` - Darker variant for headers and emphasis
- **Accent Orange**: `#E67E22` - Warm orange for CTAs and highlights
- **Accent Hover**: `#D35400` - Darker orange for hover states

### Neutral Palette
- **White**: `#FFFFFF` - Card backgrounds, primary text on dark
- **Gray 50**: `#F8FAFC` - Section alternating backgrounds
- **Gray 100**: `#F1F5F9` - Light borders, subtle backgrounds
- **Gray 200**: `#E2E8F0` - Borders, dividers
- **Gray 300**: `#CBD5E1` - Muted elements
- **Gray 500**: `#64748B` - Secondary text
- **Gray 700**: `#334155` - Body text
- **Gray 900**: `#0F172A` - Headings, primary text

### Status Colors
- **Success Green**: `#16A34A` - Success states, verification badges
- **Warning Yellow**: `#EAB308` - Warning states
- **Error Red**: `#DC2626` - Error states

## Typography

### Fonts
- **Primary**: Inter (Google Fonts)
- **Weights**: 300 (light), 400 (regular), 500 (medium), 600 (semibold), 700 (bold), 800 (extrabold)

### Scale
- **H1**: text-4xl md:text-5xl lg:text-6xl font-bold
- **H2**: text-3xl md:text-4xl font-bold
- **H3**: text-xl md:text-2xl font-semibold
- **H4**: text-lg font-semibold
- **Body**: text-base leading-relaxed
- **Small**: text-sm
- **Caption**: text-xs

## Spacing
- **Section Padding**: py-16 md:py-20 lg:py-24
- **Container**: max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8
- **Card Padding**: p-6 md:p-8
- **Grid Gap**: gap-6 md:gap-8

## Borders & Shadows
- **Card Border**: border border-gray-100
- **Card Shadow**: shadow-sm hover:shadow-md transition-shadow
- **Button Radius**: rounded-lg
- **Card Radius**: rounded-xl

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: bg-accent-orange text-white hover:bg-accent-orange-hover
- **Secondary**: bg-primary-blue text-white hover:bg-primary-dark

### Cards
- **Standard**: bg-white rounded-xl shadow-sm border border-gray-100 p-6

### Navigation
- **Header**: bg-white shadow-sm sticky top-0 z-50
- **Active Link**: text-primary-blue font-semibold
- **Hover**: text-accent-orange

## Do's
- Use Inter font consistently across all pages
- Maintain generous whitespace between sections
- Use professional stock images of factories, shipping, quality control
- Keep CTAs prominent and above the fold
- Use trust signals (logos, certifications, statistics)
- Ensure mobile-first responsive design

## Don'ts
- Don't use bright or flashy colors
- Don't use decorative fonts
- Don't overcrowd sections with too much text
- Don't use low-quality or generic stock images
- Don't use exaggerated claims or marketing fluff
- Don't mix too many font weights in one section
