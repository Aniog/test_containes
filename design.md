# Visual Identity: Velmora Fine Jewelry

## Mood
- Quiet luxury, warm, editorial.
- Premium demi-fine jewelry.
- Generous whitespace, elegant typography.

## Colors
- **Primary (Sand/Cream)**: `#FAF9F6` (Editorial background) - Tailwind: `bg-cream`
- **Secondary (Charcoal)**: `#1A1A1A` (Elegant text) - Tailwind: `text-charcoal`
- **Accent (Gold)**: `#C5A059` (Metallic accents) - Tailwind: `text-gold`, `bg-gold`
- **Muted (Taupe)**: `#E5E4E2` (Hairline dividers, soft borders) - Tailwind: `border-taupe`

## Typography
- **Headings & Product Names**: `Cormorant Garamond`, serif. Uppercase with wide letter-spacing for product names.
- **Body & UI**: `Inter`, sans-serif.

## Visual Elements
- Hairline dividers (0.5px or 1px border).
- Large editorial imagery.
- Subtle hover transitions (opacity, scale).
- Soft shadows for cards.
- Solid gold or outlined black buttons.

## Tailwind Classes (Examples)
- Serif Heading: `font-serif text-charcoal tracking-tight`
- Product Name: `font-serif text-charcoal uppercase tracking-[0.2em] text-sm`
- Accent Button: `bg-gold text-white px-8 py-3 hover:bg-opacity-90 transition-all font-sans uppercase tracking-widest text-xs`
- Divider: `border-b border-taupe w-full`
