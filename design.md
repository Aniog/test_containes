# SSourcing China - Design System

## Typography
- Font: Inter (Google Fonts), weights 300-800
- Headings: font-bold, tracking-tight
- Body: font-normal, text-base leading-relaxed
- Section titles: text-3xl md:text-4xl font-bold text-brand-900
- Section subtitles: text-lg text-slate-500 max-w-2xl mx-auto

## Colors
- Primary: Navy blue (brand-600: #1e3a8a) - headers, CTAs, key elements
- Accent: Warm orange (accent-500: #ed7d18) - CTA buttons, highlights
- Background: White (#ffffff) - clean, professional
- Section alternates: slate-50 (#f8fafc) - alternating sections
- Text: slate-900 (#0f172a) - body text
- Muted: slate-500 (#64748b) - secondary text

## Spacing
- Section padding: py-16 md:py-24
- Container: max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8
- Card padding: p-6 md:p-8
- Gap between grid items: gap-8

## Visual Style
- Clean, professional B2B look
- White backgrounds with subtle slate-50 alternating sections
- Blue/navy for trust and authority
- Orange for CTAs and highlights
- Rounded corners on cards (rounded-xl)
- Subtle shadows on cards (shadow-sm or shadow-md)
- Icons from Lucide React for visual elements
- Stock images for factory, QC, shipping visuals
- No animations or gimmicks - keep it professional

## Components
- Cards: bg-white rounded-xl shadow-sm border border-slate-100 p-6
- Buttons: rounded-lg font-semibold
  - Primary: bg-brand-600 text-white hover:bg-brand-700
  - CTA: bg-accent-500 text-white hover:bg-accent-600
  - Outline: border-2 border-brand-600 text-brand-600 hover:bg-brand-600 hover:text-white
- Section headers: text-center mb-12 md:mb-16
- Grid: grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-3 gap-8

## Do's
- Use navy blue for trust elements
- Use orange sparingly for CTAs
- Keep sections well-spaced
- Use real-looking factory/QC/shipping imagery
- Maintain clean white space

## Don'ts
- No gradients or flashy effects
- No exaggerated claims
- No dark backgrounds
- No stock photos that look fake
- No animations on scroll
