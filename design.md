# SSourcing China — Design System

## Brand Identity
A professional B2B sourcing agent based in China, connecting global buyers with reliable Chinese manufacturers. The visual identity conveys trust, reliability, international expertise, and industrial competence.

## Typography
- **Font Family**: Inter (Google Fonts), sans-serif fallback
- **Headings**: font-bold, tracking-tight
  - H1: text-4xl md:text-5xl lg:text-6xl
  - H2: text-3xl md:text-4xl
  - H3: text-xl md:text-2xl
- **Body**: text-base md:text-lg, text-gray-700
- **Small/Caption**: text-sm, text-gray-500

## Colors
- **Primary Navy**: #1B3A5C (navy-600) — headers, footer, primary buttons
- **Primary Navy Dark**: #0f2237 (navy-800) — hover states
- **Accent Gold**: #C8963E (gold-500) — CTAs, highlights, accent elements
- **Green**: #2D6A4F (green-500) — success badges, trust indicators
- **White**: #FFFFFF — cards, sections
- **Light Gray**: #F8FAFC — alternating section backgrounds
- **Text Primary**: #1e293b (slate-800)
- **Text Secondary**: #475569 (slate-600)
- **Text Muted**: #94a3b8 (slate-400)

## Layout
- Max content width: max-w-7xl (1280px), centered with mx-auto
- Section padding: py-16 md:py-20 lg:py-24
- Card border: border border-gray-100, rounded-xl, shadow-sm
- Consistent px-4 md:px-6 lg:px-8 for content padding

## Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: bg-gold-500 hover:bg-gold-600 text-white font-semibold px-6 py-3 rounded-lg transition-colors
- **Secondary**: bg-navy-600 hover:bg-navy-800 text-white font-semibold px-6 py-3 rounded-lg transition-colors
- **Outline**: border-2 border-navy-600 text-navy-600 hover:bg-navy-600 hover:text-white font-semibold px-6 py-3 rounded-lg transition-colors

## Icons
- Use Lucide React icons throughout
- Icon size: w-6 h-6 for inline, w-10 h-10 or w-12 h-12 for feature blocks
- Icon colors: navy-600, gold-500, or green-500 depending on context

## Spacing
- Section gap: space-y-16 md:space-y-20
- Card grid gap: gap-6 md:gap-8
- Content spacing: space-y-4 for paragraphs, space-y-6 for grouped content

## Do's
- Use ample white space for clean, professional look
- Use navy as the dominant color, gold sparingly for CTAs
- Use realistic imagery (factory, QC, shipping, trade)
- Keep text concise and scannable
- Use icons to reinforce key points
- Maintain consistent section padding

## Don'ts
- No bright or playful colors
- No exaggerated claims or hype language
- No dark backgrounds for body text sections
- No cluttered layouts
- No rounded full (pill) buttons for primary CTAs
- No gradients on buttons
