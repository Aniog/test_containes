# Design Guidelines - SSourcing China

## Visual Style
SSourcing China is a professional B2B sourcing agency. The design should evoke trust, transparency, and efficiency. It should look like an international B2B platform rather than a cheap factory site.

### Colors
- **Primary (Navy Blue)**: `#1E3A8A` (Tailwind `blue-900`) - Represents stability and professionalism.
- **Secondary (Gold/Amber)**: `#D97706` (Tailwind `amber-600`) - Used for CTAs to suggest value and success.
- **Accent (Light Gray)**: `#F3F4F6` (Tailwind `gray-100`) - For background sections to provide a clean, modern look.
- **Text (Slate)**: `#1F2937` (Tailwind `slate-800`) - For high readability.
- **Success (Green)**: `#059669` (Tailwind `emerald-600`) - For verification and quality checks.

### Typography
- **Primary Font**: 'Inter', sans-serif.
- **Headings**: Semi-bold to Bold, slate-900.
- **Body**: Regular, slate-700, line-height 1.6.

### Spacing & Layout
- Use generous whitespace to avoid a cluttered look.
- Section padding: `py-16` or `py-20` for desktop.
- Max-width containers: `max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4`.
- Grid-based layouts for services and features.

### Components Visuals
- **Cards**: Subtle shadows (`shadow-sm` or `shadow-md`), rounded corners (`rounded-lg`).
- **Buttons**: Rounded-md, bold text, clear contrast.
- **Images**: High-quality, realistic images of factories, quality inspections, and logistics. No generic "handshake" stock photos if avoidable.
- **Icons**: Lucide React - clean, thin lines.

### Do's
- Use realistic imagery of China sourcing operations.
- Maintain a clean, professional B2B aesthetic.
- Use clear, benefit-driven headlines.
- Ensure all text is highly readable against backgrounds.

### Don'ts
- Use overly bright or neon colors.
- Use cluttered or busy backgrounds.
- Use low-resolution or generic-looking stock photos.
- Use small font sizes for body text.
