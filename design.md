# Velmora Fine Jewelry - Design System

## Brand Personality
- **Mood**: Quiet luxury, warm, editorial, sophisticated, timeless.
- **Target**: Women 25–45 looking for premium but accessible demi-fine jewelry.

## Color Palette
- **Primary (Background)**: Ivory/Sand (#FDFCFB) - a soft, warm neutral that glows.
- **Secondary (Text)**: Obsidian (#1A1A1A) - for sharp, elegant typography.
- **Accent (Metallic)**: Gold Dust (#D4AF37) - used sparingly for buttons and highlights.
- **Muted**: Stone (#717171) - for secondary text and borders.

## Typography
- **Headings & Product Names**: Cormorant Garamond (Serif).
  - Use `uppercase` and `tracking-wider` for product titles.
- **Body & UI**: Manrope (Sans-serif).
  - Clean, modern, highly legible.

## UI Elements
- **Dividers**: Thin hairlines (0.5px or 1px) in muted stone.
- **Buttons**:
  - Primary: Solid Gold Dust or Obsidian with white text.
  - Secondary: Outlined with thin border.
- **Whitespace**: Highly generous to create an editorial, high-end feel.
- **Shadows**: Extremely soft, almost imperceptible.

## Tailwind Classes (Examples)
- Text: `text-stone-900` (Obsidian), `text-stone-500` (Stone).
- Background: `bg-[#FDFCFB]`.
- Font: `font-serif` (Cormorant Garamond), `font-sans` (Manrope).
