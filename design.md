# Visual Style Guide - SSourcing China

## Typography
- Main Font: Inter, sans-serif
- Headings: Semi-bold to bold (font-weight: 600, 700)
- Body: Regular (font-weight: 400)

## Colors (Tailwind classes)
- Primary (Navy/Professional Blue): `bg-slate-900`, `text-slate-900`, `border-slate-900`
- Accent (Blue): `bg-blue-600`, `text-blue-600`
- Background: `bg-white`, `bg-slate-50`
- Text: `text-slate-900` (headings), `text-slate-600` (body)
- Success/CTA: `bg-blue-600`, `hover:bg-blue-700`

## Components
- Cards: Simple with light borders (`border-slate-200`), light shadows (`shadow-sm`)
- Buttons: Rounded corners (`rounded-md`), professional padding (`px-4 py-2`)
- Input Fields: Clear borders, focus states with blue ring

## Do's
- Use high-quality, realistic images related to factory inspections, quality control, and shipping.
- Maintain consistent white space.
- Use clear, practical icons (Lucide React).

## Don'ts
- Avoid exaggerated claims or "get rich quick" vibes.
- No overly bright or flashy colors that detract from professionalism.
- Avoid cluttered layouts.
