# Velmora Fine Jewelry — Design System

## Brand Direction
Quiet luxury, warm editorial, premium-but-accessible demi-fine jewelry. The feel should be restrained, confident, and tactile — like a high-end jewelry boutique with soft lighting.

## Color Palette
- **Base / Background:** `#F6F3EE` warm parchment
- **Surface / Card:** `#FFFFFF` clean white
- **Text Primary:** `#1C1917` warm black
- **Text Secondary:** `#78716C` warm stone
- **Accent / Gold:** `#C5A059` muted metallic gold
- **Accent Hover:** `#A8863F` deeper gold
- **Border / Divider:** `#E7E5E4` warm gray
- **Success:** `#4A7C59` muted green
- **Error:** `#B91C1C` restrained red

## Typography
- **Headings / Product Names:** Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display, Georgia, serif
  - Product names: UPPERCASE, wide letter-spacing, elegant weight
- **Body / UI:** Inter, Manrope, system-ui, sans-serif
- **Scale:** generous line-height, comfortable measure, restrained weight contrast

## Spacing & Layout
- Generous whitespace, especially on mobile
- Thin hairline dividers (`border border-stone-200`)
- Soft shadows (`shadow-sm`, `shadow-md`)
- Rounded corners: `rounded-sm` to `rounded-md` only
- Max content width: `max-w-7xl` with consistent side padding

## Components
- Buttons: solid accent for primary, outlined accent for secondary
- Cards: white background, subtle shadow, thin border
- Images: warm editorial feel, soft transitions on hover
- Forms: minimal borders, generous padding, clear labels

## Motion
- Subtle hover transitions (`transition-all duration-300`)
- Soft fade/slide for drawers and modals
- No bouncy or flashy animations

## Accessibility
- Strong contrast ratios for all text
- Visible focus states
- Semantic HTML and ARIA labels where needed
- Touch-friendly tap targets on mobile
