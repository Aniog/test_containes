# Velmora Fine Jewelry Visual System

Velmora uses a quiet luxury editorial direction: deep espresso foundations, warm ivory surfaces, and restrained antique-gold accents that flatter demi-fine gold jewelry without looking loud or discount-driven.

## Palette
- Deep base: velmora-espresso (`#241712`) for premium dark sections and text.
- Soft page: velmora-ivory (`#F8F1E8`) for the primary background.
- Warm card: velmora-porcelain (`#FFF9F1`) for product cards, forms, and elevated panels.
- Muted text: velmora-taupe (`#7A695D`) for secondary copy with strong contrast on light surfaces.
- Accent: velmora-gold (`#B8894D`) and velmora-goldDeep (`#8A6538`) for CTAs, stars, borders, and subtle highlights.
- Hairlines: velmora-line (`#E2D3C0`) for thin dividers.

## Typography
- Headings and product names: Cormorant Garamond, elegant serif.
- Body and UI: Manrope, clean sans-serif.
- Product names should be uppercase with wide letter-spacing.

## Layout and Details
- Use generous whitespace, wide editorial sections, large imagery, thin dividers, and soft shadows.
- Buttons should feel premium: solid antique gold or refined outlined accents.
- Use subtle transitions: opacity, transform, and color changes only.
- Avoid bright sale colors, generic ecommerce blue, heavy borders, loud gradients, and cluttered layouts.

## Tailwind Examples
- Page background: `bg-velmora-ivory text-velmora-espresso`
- Card: `bg-velmora-porcelain text-velmora-espresso border border-velmora-line shadow-soft`
- Accent button: `bg-velmora-gold text-white hover:bg-velmora-goldDeep`
- Hairline: `border-velmora-line`
