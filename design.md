# SSourcing China - Design System

## Brand Identity
SSourcing China is a professional B2B sourcing agency. The design must convey trust, reliability, professionalism, and international capability.

## Colors
- **Primary (Brand)**: Deep navy blue (#1e40af brand-600) - Trust, professionalism, corporate
- **Accent**: Warm orange (#c2410c accent-500) - Energy, action, CTA buttons
- **Neutrals**: Light gray (#f8fafc neutral-50) backgrounds, dark slate (#0f172a neutral-900) for headings
- **Text**: #334155 neutral-700 for body text, #0f172a neutral-900 for headings
- **Surfaces**: White (#ffffff) cards, neutral-50 page backgrounds

## Typography
- **Font**: Inter (sans-serif) throughout
- **Headings**: Bold (700), dark, generous letter-spacing for impact
- **Body**: Regular (400), 16px base, comfortable line-height 1.6
- **Section Titles**: 30px/36px (mobile/desktop), bold
- **Small text**: 14px, neutral-500 for metadata

## Spacing
- **Section padding**: py-16 (mobile), py-24 (desktop)
- **Card padding**: p-6 or p-8
- **Grid gaps**: gap-8 between cards
- **Content max-width**: 1280px (max-w-7xl)

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: btn-primary (brand-600 bg, white text, rounded-lg)
- **Accent CTA**: btn-accent (accent-500 bg, white text) - for "Get a Free Quote"
- **Outline**: btn-outline (brand border, brand text, hover fill)

### Cards
- White background, rounded-xl, shadow-sm, border border-neutral-100
- Hover: shadow-md, subtle lift transform

### Navigation
- Fixed top header, white bg, subtle bottom border
- Desktop: horizontal links, CTA button on right
- Mobile: hamburger menu with slide-down panel

### Forms
- Clean inputs with border, rounded-lg, focus ring brand-500
- Labels above inputs, neutral-600
- Submit button with accent color

## Visual Style
- Clean, minimal, lots of whitespace
- Professional photography of factories, QC inspections, shipping containers
- Icons from Lucide for service illustrations
- Gradients: brand-600 to brand-800 for hero overlays
- Borders: neutral-200 for subtle separators
- Section backgrounds alternate between white and neutral-50

## Layout
- Full-width sections with max-w-7xl content containers
- 12-column grid system via Tailwind
- Responsive breakpoints: sm (640), md (768), lg (1024), xl (1280)
- Mobile: single column, stacked layout
- Tablet: 2-column grids
- Desktop: 3-4 column grids

## Do's
- Use consistent brand colors
- Keep plenty of whitespace
- Use professional, high-quality imagery
- Make CTAs prominent with accent color
- Use clear hierarchy in headings
- Keep forms simple and clean

## Don'ts
- Don't use overly bright or playful colors
- Don't crowd elements together
- Don't use casual language or emojis
- Don't make exaggerated claims
- Don't use low-contrast text
