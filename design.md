# SSourcing China Design System

## Brand direction
A clean, trustworthy, international B2B website for overseas buyers evaluating a China sourcing partner. The design should feel practical, transparent, and well-organized rather than flashy.

## Visual language
- Use a bright neutral background with navy and slate foundations.
- Use teal as the primary accent for calls to action and process highlights.
- Use soft card surfaces and subtle borders instead of heavy shadows.
- Photography should show realistic factory audits, product inspection, supplier meetings, production oversight, container loading, and shipping coordination.
- Keep layouts spacious and highly readable.

## Suggested Tailwind style tokens
- Page background: `bg-slate-50`
- Primary text: `text-slate-900`
- Secondary text: `text-slate-600`
- Section surface: `bg-white`
- Muted surface: `bg-slate-100`
- Primary brand surface: `bg-slate-900`
- Primary accent: `bg-teal-600`
- Primary accent hover: `hover:bg-teal-700`
- Accent text on dark backgrounds: `text-white`
- Borders: `border-slate-200`
- Highlight badge surface: `bg-teal-50`
- Highlight badge text: `text-teal-700`

## Typography
- Use Inter.
- Main hero headline: `text-4xl md:text-5xl lg:text-6xl font-semibold tracking-tight`
- Section headings: `text-3xl md:text-4xl font-semibold tracking-tight`
- Eyebrow labels: `text-sm font-semibold uppercase tracking-[0.2em]`
- Body text: `text-base md:text-lg leading-7`
- Card titles: `text-xl font-semibold`

## Spacing
- Page sections: `py-16 md:py-24`
- Standard container: `mx-auto w-full max-w-7xl px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8`
- Card padding: `p-6 md:p-8`
- Button height: `h-11 md:h-12`

## Components
- Buttons should have rounded corners, strong contrast, and clear hover states.
- Cards should use `rounded-3xl border border-slate-200 bg-white` with minimal shadow like `shadow-sm` only when useful.
- Use grid layouts on desktop for service cards, product categories, and case studies.
- Use sticky header with clear CTA.
- Forms should use white surfaces, slate text, slate placeholders, and visible focus states.

## Do
- Keep all text clearly legible against the background.
- Use concise, practical copy aimed at procurement teams, importers, and brand owners.
- Prefer calm, structured sections over dense marketing blocks.
- Maintain clear visual hierarchy and conversion-focused CTAs.

## Don’t
- Do not use exaggerated claims, loud gradients, or overly decorative effects.
- Do not use low-contrast text on cards, forms, or colored surfaces.
- Do not stack the entire desktop layout into a narrow mobile-style single column when wider layouts are appropriate.
