# Velmora Fine Jewelry Design System

## Typography
- **Headings & Product Names**: Cormorant Garamond (Serif)
  - Style: Elegant, uppercase for product names, wide letter-spacing.
  - Tailwind: `font-serif tracking-[0.2em] uppercase`
- **Body & UI**: Inter / Manrope (Sans-serif)
  - Style: Clean, readable.
  - Tailwind: `font-sans`

## Palette
- **Primary (Background)**: `#FAFAFA` (Off-white/Refined paper)
- **Secondary (Accents)**: `#C5A059` (Soft Gold / Muted metallic)
- **Dark (Text)**: `#1A1A1A` (Deep Charcoal)
- **Dividers/Borders**: `#E5E5E5` (Thin hairline)
- **Muted**: `#717171` (Gray for subtext)

## UI Elements
- **Buttons**:
  - Primary: Solid `#1A1A1A` with white text, or solid `#C5A059`. Small, refined padding.
  - Secondary: Outlined thin border, hover subtle bg fill.
- **Transitions**: Ease-in-out, 300ms.
- **Shadows**: Very soft, large blur (elevated editorial look).

## Guidelines
- **Generous Whitespace**: Minimum `py-12` for sections, `gap-8` for grids.
- **Large Imagery**: High-quality focused shots with warm lighting.
- **Thin Hairline Dividers**: Use `border-t border-hairline` where hairline is `0.5px`.
