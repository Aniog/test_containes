# Velmora Fine Jewelry - Design System

## Visual Identity
- Mood: quiet luxury, warm, editorial
- Premium demi-fine jewelry — NOT loud, NOT discount-looking, NOT generic e-commerce

## Color Palette
- Background: `#faf8f5` (warm cream)
- Surface: `#ffffff` (white)
- Primary text: `#1a1a1a` (warm charcoal)
- Secondary text: `#6b6560` (warm gray)
- Muted text: `#8a8178` (muted warm gray)
- Accent/Gold: `#c9a96e` (warm metallic gold)
- Accent hover: `#b8944f` (darker gold)
- Border: `#e8e4df` (light warm gray)
- Section bg: `#f5f0eb` (soft blush)
- Dark section: `#1a1a1a` (charcoal)
- Dark text on dark: `#faf8f5` (cream)

## Typography
- Headings: Cormorant Garamond (serif) - elegant, editorial
- Body/UI: Inter (sans-serif) - clean, modern
- Product names: UPPERCASE with wide letter-spacing (tracking-widest)

## Spacing
- Generous whitespace throughout
- Section padding: py-20 to py-28
- Container max-width: max-w-7xl

## Components
- Buttons: solid gold or outlined, rounded-sm, uppercase tracking-wider
- Cards: subtle hover lift, soft shadow
- Dividers: thin hairline (border-t border-[#e8e4df])
- Accordions: clean, minimal with chevron icons

## Animations
- Subtle hover transitions (duration-300, ease-out)
- Soft fade-ins
- Smooth scroll behavior
